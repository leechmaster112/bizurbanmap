Stonehaven Dental

Our Spanish Fork, Utah, practice is led by two doctors and a hard-working team that loves taking care of patients and making them feel comfortable and cared for. We are conveniently located off U.S. 6, on North 920 E, near VASA Fitness. In addition to having Spanish-speaking team members, we offer numerous general dentistry and specialty services, including cosmetic dentistry, clear aligners, dental implants, sedation and orthodontics. Stonehaven Dental was founded as a single practice by Dr. Bret Tobler and his two sons nearly 30 years ago. Today, Stonehaven Dental operates multiple practices in Utah.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What services does Stonehaven Dental offer?

We provide a comprehensive range of dental services, including preventative care like cleanings and exams, restorative treatments such as fillings and crowns, and cosmetic procedures like teeth whitening. Our focus is on delivering personalized care to meet the specific needs of each patient.

Q: Do you accept new patients?

Yes, we welcome new patients at our Spanish Fork location. To schedule your initial consultation, please call our office at (801) 794-3567.

Q: What is the cost of a dental cleaning?

The cost of a dental cleaning can vary based on individual needs and insurance coverage. We recommend calling our office for a personalized estimate.

Q: Do you offer emergency dental care?

While we primarily schedule appointments, we can often accommodate dental emergencies. Please call our office for guidance.

Q: How often should I schedule dental check-ups?

We generally recommend scheduling dental check-ups every six months to maintain optimal oral health. However, the frequency may vary based on individual needs.
