Frequently Asked Questions

1. What services do you offer for routine pet care?

We provide comprehensive routine care including annual check-ups, vaccinations, dental cleanings, parasite control, and preventive health plans tailored to your pet's specific needs.

2. Do you offer emergency veterinary services?

Yes, our urgent care services are available for unexpected health issues. We recommend calling ahead for the best care pathway during non-emergency situations.

3. Can I schedule an appointment for my pet?

We recommend scheduling appointments to ensure prompt service. Walk-ins are welcome when space is available, but calling ahead is appreciated.

4. What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, credit cards, and debit cards. We also offer payment plans for major services upon request.

5. Do you provide pet boarding services?

At this location, we focus on veterinary and urgent care services. For boarding services, please contact our main Petfolk location.
