Frequently Asked Questions

What type of Chinese food do you offer? We offer a standard selection of Chinese dishes including popular favorites like Kung Pao Chicken, Mapo Tofu, and various rice and noodle dishes. We also provide combo plates for convenient and economical meals.

How can I place an order? You can place your order by calling our phone number (757) 938-9158. We accept cash and credit/debit cards at the counter.

Do you have vegetarian options? Yes, we offer several vegetarian dishes such as Mapo Tofu, Vegetable Chow Mein, and various rice dishes. Please inform your server if you require specific dietary accommodations.

Is the restaurant primarily counter-service? Yes, SuperWok operates as a counter-service restaurant. You will order and pay at the front counter, and your food will be prepared and served promptly.
