Boomcycle Digital Marketing

Boomcycle Digital Marketing, founded by David Victor in 2003, is a San Francisco Bay Area-based, full-service digital marketing agency headquartered in San Ramon, California, with additional offices in Pleasanton, renowned for over two decades of pioneering innovative strategies that drive measurable business growth through services like search engine optimization (SEO), pay-per-click (PPC) advertising via Google Ads, intelligent web design, and Google Business Profile optimization. Specializing in ethical "white hat" SEO and cutting-edge techniques, Boomcycle helps businesses across industries—such as biotech, healthcare, financial services, music, and technology—boost online visibility, attract qualified leads, and increase revenue, leveraging proprietary tools like its Marketing Intelligence System for advanced analytics and SEO Hyper-Optimization for precision content alignment with search algorithms. Victor, a Cal State East Bay Computer Science graduate and former member of the multi-platinum band BOSTON, brings a unique blend of technical expertise and creative marketing insight, having grown the agency from its first project for a Saratoga day spa into a trusted partner for local, regional, and national clients, delivering standout results like a 200% organic traffic surge for Danville Music and a $212,000 Google Ads revenue boost with a 10x return on ad spend. With a concierge-style approach, a team of seasoned experts, and a commitment to transparency and tailored strategies, Boomcycle empowers businesses to dominate search rankings, enhance their digital presence, and achieve lasting success.
