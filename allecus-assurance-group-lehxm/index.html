Allecus Assurance Group

Allecus Assurance Group provides non-medical home care services in Tuscaloosa, Birmingham, and surrounding Alabama communities. Services include personal care, companion care, respite care, dementia care, veteran and specialized care, and homemaker services. Founded on a foundation of quality care built to last for generations, AAG caregivers are trained under 40+ years of nursing leadership to meet individuals where they are — flexible, resilient, and diligent in ensuring quality of life. Serving Tuscaloosa, Northport, Birmingham, Hoover, Vestavia Hills, and surrounding areas. Medicaid and private pay accepted. Call (877) 786-8088 for a free consultation.
