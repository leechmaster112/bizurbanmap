Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is a sober living house?

A sober living house, like the one we provide in New York, is a residence designed for individuals in recovery from substance abuse. It offers a structured, drug-free environment, peer support, and a focus on maintaining sobriety. It serves as a crucial transitional step between intensive treatment and independent living.

Q2: Who is eligible to live at your sober living house in New York?

We typically accept men who are actively engaged in their recovery, often transitioning from inpatient rehabilitation programs in New York or outpatient treatment. We look for individuals committed to sobriety, willing to follow house rules, and ready to participate in the recovery community.

Q3: What kind of support do you offer?

We provide a supportive community environment, regular house meetings, and a framework for personal recovery work. While we are not a treatment facility, we encourage and support the use of resources like therapy and 12-step programs as part of an individual's overall recovery plan.

Q4: What are the rules and expectations?

Our house rules are centered around maintaining a sober and safe environment. This includes strict sobriety, adherence to a schedule, participation in house chores and meetings, and respect for housemates. Specific rules may be outlined during the application process.

You can also call us directly at (267) 412-3425 to speak with our team about the application process and determine if our program is the right fit for your journey to recovery in New York.
