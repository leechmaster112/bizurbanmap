Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of sewer line problems do you repair?

We handle a wide range of issues, including pipe collapses, cracks and breaks, tree root intrusion, drain field failures, clogs, and backups. Our focus is on diagnosing the specific problem and providing the most effective solution.

Q2: How quickly can you respond to an emergency sewer issue?

We understand that sewer line emergencies can happen unexpectedly. While we aim to accommodate all customers, our priority is to respond as quickly as possible to urgent situations in the Coral Gables area. Please call us immediately at (786) 460-6891.

Q3: Do you offer trenchless sewer repair services?

Yes, absolutely. We specialize in trenchless pipe lining (CIPP) technology. This method repairs damaged pipes from the inside without the need for extensive digging, which is often less disruptive and faster than traditional methods.

Q4: How long does a typical sewer line repair take?

The duration depends on the complexity of the problem and the chosen repair method. Minor repairs might take a few hours, while more extensive work could require a day or two. Our team will provide you with a realistic timeline once the assessment is complete.

Q5: Do I need to be home during the sewer repair process?

It depends on the scope of the work. For many services, especially trenchless repairs, you may not need to be present. However, for larger projects involving excavation, we will coordinate with you for access and updates. Our team will keep you informed throughout the process.
