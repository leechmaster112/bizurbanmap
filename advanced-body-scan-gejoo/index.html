Frequently Asked Questions

1. What exactly does a full body scan at Advanced Body Scan detect?

Our full body scan is designed to provide a comprehensive overview of your internal health. It can help identify potential issues such as abnormalities in organs, signs of early-stage diseases (like certain cancers), bone density concerns, and other structural irregularities. It serves as a baseline or monitoring tool for overall health.

2. How long does an Advanced Body Scan appointment typically take?

Most full body scans at our Plano location take approximately 10-15 minutes to complete. However, the total time you spend at our facility, including preparation and brief review of preliminary findings with our staff, may be closer to 30-45 minutes. Heart scans are generally quicker, often completed within 5-10 minutes.

3. Are the scans safe? What technology do you use?

Yes, our scanning procedures are considered safe. We utilize advanced imaging technology, typically low-dose computed tomography (CT) for both full body and heart scans, which is designed to minimize radiation exposure while still providing high-quality diagnostic images. Our protocols prioritize patient safety.

4. Do I need a referral to get a scan done at Advanced Body Scan?

While a referral is not strictly mandatory for walk-in appointments, we strongly recommend discussing your specific health needs with your primary care physician. They can advise if a scan is appropriate for you and help interpret the results in the context of your overall medical history.

5. How quickly will I receive my scan results after my appointment?

Our team processes results promptly. Typically, you can expect to receive your preliminary findings via email within 24-48 business hours following your scan at our Plano location. Your images and detailed report will also be available online through your secure patient portal. A full report with detailed interpretation will be forwarded to your referring physician.
