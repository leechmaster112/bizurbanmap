Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of employment law issues do you handle?

We specialize in employment law issues for employees in Toronto. This includes, but is not limited to, claims related to wrongful dismissal, termination without cause, insufficient notice or pay in lieu of notice, severance agreements, workplace harassment, discrimination, defamation, and disputes regarding entitlements like vacation pay, overtime, and statutory holiday pay.

Q2: How long do I have to file a claim for unfair dismissal?

In Ontario, the limitation period for filing a lawsuit related to termination or dismissal is generally two years from the date you became aware of the act that forms the basis of your claim. It's crucial to seek legal advice promptly to understand your specific situation and deadlines.

Q3: Can you help me understand my contract or offer letter?

Yes, we can review your employment contract, offer letter, or termination agreement to explain your rights and obligations under Ontario law. We can advise you on issues like notice periods, pay, non-compete clauses, and ensure the agreement is fair and complies with the Employment Standards Act.

Q4: What should I do if I'm being harassed at work?

If you are experiencing workplace harassment, it is important to document the incidents in detail, including dates, times, locations, witnesses, and what was said or done. Report the harassment through your employer's formal procedures as outlined in your employee handbook or directly to Human Resources. We can advise you on the best course of action and help you understand your rights.
