Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of employment issues do you handle for clients in Toronto?

We primarily handle issues such as wrongful dismissal, termination without cause, disputes over employment contracts, workplace harassment and discrimination claims, pay deductions, and constructive dismissal claims for employees residing and working in Toronto.

Q2: How long do I have to file a claim for unfair dismissal in Toronto?

In Ontario, employees generally have two years from the date the dismissal occurred to file a claim for termination without cause or other employment-related issues. Prompt action is often advisable to preserve evidence and rights.

Q3: Do I need a lawyer for my workplace harassment case in Toronto?

While you have the right to pursue a claim without legal representation, having an experienced employment lawyer significantly increases your chances of a successful outcome. We provide comprehensive legal support for such cases in Toronto.

Q4: Can you help me with drafting a new employment contract in Toronto?

Yes, we assist employers and employees in drafting, reviewing, and negotiating employment contracts to ensure they are fair, comprehensive, and compliant with Ontario law.

Q5: What is the typical process for resolving an employment dispute in Toronto?

The process varies by case but often involves an initial consultation, investigation of facts, potential negotiation with the employer, and if necessary, formal legal proceedings such as filing a Statement of Claim with the Ontario Superior Court of Justice.
