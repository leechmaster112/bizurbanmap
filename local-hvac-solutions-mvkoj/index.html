Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What types of HVAC systems do you install?

A: We install a variety of HVAC systems suitable for homes and businesses in New York, including central air conditioning units, heat pumps, gas and electric furnaces, and packaged terminal air conditioners.

Q2: How long does an HVAC system typically last?

A: With proper maintenance, most HVAC systems can last between 10 to 15 years. Signs that it might be time for a replacement include increased energy bills, inconsistent temperatures, unusual noises, and frequent breakdowns.

Q3: When should I schedule preventative maintenance?

A: We recommend scheduling preventative maintenance for your HVAC system at least once a year, typically before the start of the heating and cooling seasons. Regular maintenance helps maintain efficiency and prevent unexpected failures.

Q4: Are your technicians licensed and insured?

A: Absolutely. All our technicians are fully licensed, certified, and insured. We adhere to all local regulations and safety standards to ensure quality service and peace of mind for our customers.

Q5: What is your emergency service policy?

A: We understand that HVAC emergencies can happen anytime. Our emergency service policy ensures that we respond promptly to urgent repair requests outside of regular business hours, providing the quickest resolution possible.
