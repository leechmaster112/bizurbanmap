Understanding NeuroStar TMS Therapy

Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) is a therapeutic technique that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain. The NeuroStar system, the platform we utilize at our Franklin location, is FDA-approved for the treatment of major depressive disorder, particularly for individuals who have not found sufficient relief from traditional antidepressant medications.

Unlike invasive procedures, NeuroStar TMS is completely non-invasive. It does not require surgery or anesthesia. During a typical session, our trained technicians will use a device that includes a comfortable treatment coil placed gently against your head. This coil generates precisely measured magnetic pulses that target specific areas of your brain involved in depression.

The treatment is generally well-tolerated, with most patients reporting sensations such as a tapping or tingling feeling where the coil is applied. Our team at TMS of Tennessee Franklin is experienced in ensuring your comfort throughout the process. The NeuroStar system also incorporates ESG™ (Ergonomic Scan Geometry) technology, which is designed to optimize the magnetic field delivery and potentially enhance treatment outcomes.
