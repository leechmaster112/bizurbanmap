Red Bud Isle

Red Bud Isle is a scenic off-leash dog park on a 13-acre peninsula surrounded by Lady Bird Lake in Austin, with winding natural trails through the trees and multiple lake access points for swimming. Shaded and well-maintained with waste stations, it's a favorite for water-loving dogs. Hours, photos, and directions are on its Doggie Park Near Me listing.
