Location

The SoundLab Music School & Store, located right here in St Kilda at 1 Chapel St, VIC 3182, is your premier destination for musical education and instrument acquisition. Under the iconic Astor Theatre, we provide a unique environment where passion for music can be nurtured and instruments can be found.

At The SoundLab Music School & Store, we understand the thrill of finding the perfect instrument or embarking on a new musical journey. Our store offers a carefully curated selection of quality acoustic and electric guitars, bass guitars, keyboards, drums, and a wide range of accessories and sound equipment suitable for beginners and experienced musicians alike. Whether you're searching for a vintage find or the latest model, our knowledgeable team is here to assist you in finding the sound that resonates with you.

Our commitment extends beyond just selling instruments. The SoundLab Music School provides expert tuition for students of all ages and abilities. We offer lessons in popular instruments like guitar, bass, drums, keyboard, and voice, taught by experienced and passionate instructors. Our goal is to help you turn up your musical ability, building confidence and skill in a supportive atmosphere located right here in St Kilda. We pride ourselves on providing high-quality music education right here at 1 Chapel St.

Visit us at our St Kilda location to explore our instrument collection or to inquire about our music school programs. We are dedicated to supporting the musical community right here in our neighbourhood.
