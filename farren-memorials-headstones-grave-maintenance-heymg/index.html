Farren Memorials - Headstones & Grave Maintenance

Located at 5 Carleton Road, Marino, Dublin 3 (D03 DA58), Farren Memorials provides dedicated and respectful services for memorial upkeep and grave maintenance within our local community. We understand that maintaining the final resting place of loved ones is an important task, often filled with personal significance and requires a certain level of care and professionalism.

As a family-owned and operated business, Farren Memorials is deeply rooted in the traditions and sensitivities surrounding memorial care. We pride ourselves on offering personalised and reliable services tailored to the specific needs of families residing locally. Our commitment is to ensure that headstones remain upright, clean, and presentable, and that graves are maintained with dignity.

Our primary services encompass:

Headstone Cleaning: We offer thorough cleaning services for granite, marble, and other types of headstones found locally. Our techniques are designed to remove moss, lichen, weathering, and accumulated dirt without causing damage to the stone's surface or inscriptions.

Headstone Repair & Restoration: Minor repairs and restoration work, such as re-securing loose headstones or addressing minor damage, can be arranged through our local services.

Grave Sodding & Maintenance: We provide the planting and maintenance of grass or sod on gravesites, ensuring a neat and respectful appearance throughout the year.

Grave Decoration: Seasonal and memorial decoration services can be arranged, adhering to the wishes of the family and local customs.

Headstone Installation: Should a headstone need to be placed at 5 Carleton Road, Marino, Dublin 3, we can assist with the installation process, ensuring it is done respectfully and correctly.

Our team at Farren Memorials is committed to providing compassionate and efficient service. We understand the emotional weight associated with these tasks and strive to make the process as smooth and respectful as possible for our valued local customers.
