North 7th Street Church of Christ

North 7th Street Church of Christ - Frequently Asked Questions

1. What days and times are services held at 2205 N 7th St?

Our regular worship services are held every Sunday morning at 10:30 AM right here at our church location on North 7th Street.

2. What can I expect during a typical service at 2205 N 7th St?

You can expect a traditional service featuring scripture reading, prayer, worship music, and a message based on biblical teachings. Fellowship opportunities are available before and after the service.

3. Is there a dress code for attending services at 2205 N 7th St?

No formal dress code is enforced. We encourage you to wear whatever makes you comfortable while showing respect for the worship environment.

4. Are children welcome during services at 2205 N 7th St?

Absolutely! Children are welcome and encouraged to participate in our services. We provide a nurturing environment for all ages at our Harlingen location.

5. How can I learn more about joining the community at 2205 N 7th St?

You can learn more by visiting our church website or contacting us directly using the provided phone number. We offer various opportunities to get involved and learn more about our fellowship.
