Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What types of floral design courses do you offer?

A: We offer a range of courses including Introduction to Floral Design, Wedding & Event Design, Seasonal Bouquet Creation, Advanced Arrangement Techniques, and specialized workshops on topics like Ikebana or dried flower preservation.

Q: Can I learn to design flowers for events?

A: Yes, our curriculum includes dedicated courses and workshops focused on event floral design, covering tablescapes, centerpieces, and event-specific themes to prepare students for professional event roles.

Q: Do you provide materials for the courses?

A: Course materials and flowers are included in the tuition for most of our programs. Specific details regarding supplies will be provided when you register for a course.

Q: Are there part-time or evening classes available?

A: We strive to offer flexible scheduling, including part-time and potentially evening options for some courses. Please contact us at (713) 681-2400 or visit our website to inquire about specific class times.

Q: Can I take a single workshop or do I need to enroll in a full program?

A: We offer both individual workshops for specific skills and comprehensive certificate programs. You can choose to take single workshops or enroll in a structured program based on your learning goals.
