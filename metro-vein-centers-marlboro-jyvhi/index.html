Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What specific vein conditions do you treat at your Marlboro location?

We specialize in treating a range of venous conditions, including spider veins, varicose veins, telangiectasias, reticulated veins, and symptoms related to chronic venous insufficiency (CVI). Our treatments are tailored to the specific needs of each patient.

Q2: How long does a typical vein treatment procedure take at your center?

Most minimally invasive vein treatment procedures performed at our Marlboro center take approximately 30 to 90 minutes, depending on the complexity of the case and the specific technique used. Many patients can return to their normal activities the same day.

Q3: Is there a consultation required before scheduling a treatment?

Yes, we recommend an initial consultation at our Marlboro office. During this visit, our vascular specialists will evaluate your condition, discuss your concerns and goals, explain potential treatment options, and determine the most appropriate course of action for you.

Q4: What are the potential side effects or downtime associated with vein treatments?

While treatments are generally well-tolerated, potential side effects may include temporary bruising, swelling, redness, or numbness at the treatment site. Most patients experience minimal downtime, often able to resume normal activities immediately or within a day or two.

We understand that investing in your health is important. Please inquire during your consultation at our Marlboro location about our financing options, which may help make vein treatment accessible and affordable for our patients.
