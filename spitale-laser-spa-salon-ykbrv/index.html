Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of laser services do you offer at your Webster location?

A1: At our Spitale Laser Spa Salon location in Webster, NY, we specialize in laser hair removal, laser skin rejuvenation (including treatments for sun damage, fine lines, and texture), and laser tattoo removal.

Q2: Is laser treatment painful?

A2: While some clients experience a mild stinging or snapping sensation during the treatment, most find it tolerable. We utilize advanced cooling systems to minimize discomfort, and topical numbing creams are available upon request to ensure your comfort throughout the session.

Q3: How many laser treatment sessions are typically needed?

A3: The number of sessions required varies depending on the specific treatment, your skin type, hair color (for hair removal), and the area being treated. Your dedicated esthetician will discuss a customized treatment plan during your initial consultation.

Q4: What should I expect after a laser treatment?

A4: You may experience some redness and mild swelling immediately after your treatment, similar to a sunburn. These effects usually subside within a few hours or days. We provide post-care instructions to help manage any potential side effects and optimize your results.

Q5: Can I schedule a consultation at your Webster salon?

A5: Absolutely! You can schedule a complimentary consultation by calling our Webster location directly at (585) 256-3490. This is your opportunity to meet our team, discuss your goals, and determine if laser treatment is the right choice for you.
