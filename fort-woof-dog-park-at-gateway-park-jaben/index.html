Fort Woof Dog Park at Gateway Park

Fort Woof Dog Park is Fort Worth's first off-leash dog park, located in Gateway Park with separate fenced areas for small dogs under 40 lbs and large dogs over 40 lbs. The roughly 5-acre park offers picnic tables, benches, watering stations for dogs and humans, waste stations with bags, agility courses, and shaded shelters. Hours, photos, and directions are on its Doggie Park Near Me listing.
