FAQ

Q: What types of injuries do you commonly treat?

At Streamline Performance Physical Therapy, we commonly treat musculoskeletal injuries related to sports and daily activities. This includes sprains, strains, tendon injuries, joint pain, post-surgical rehabilitation, and performance-related issues for active adults and athletes.

Q: How long does a typical physical therapy session last?

Most initial evaluation sessions at our Phoenix location last approximately 60-75 minutes. Follow-up treatment sessions typically range from 45 to 60 minutes, depending on the complexity of your treatment plan.

Q: Do you offer treatment for athletes of all levels?

Absolutely! Our therapists have experience working with athletes ranging from weekend warriors to competitive professionals. We understand the unique demands of various sports and activities and tailor our treatments accordingly.

Q: What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring your insurance information, a referral if required by your plan, a list of medications you are taking, and comfortable clothing suitable for movement. We also recommend bringing your shoes if foot mechanics are part of your treatment.

Q: How soon can I expect to see results?

Many clients experience improvements in pain and function within the first few weeks of consistent therapy. The timeline for significant results varies based on the nature and severity of your condition, your commitment to the home exercise program, and your body's natural healing capacity.
