Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need prior experience to try axe throwing?

A: Absolutely not! We offer beginner-friendly sessions and provide all necessary instruction. Our professional coaches will guide you through the basics, ensuring you have a safe and enjoyable experience.

Q: How long does a typical axe throwing session last?

A: Standard sessions usually last around 1-2 hours, depending on the package you choose. This includes instruction, practice time, and the throwing experience itself.

Q: Is axe throwing safe?

A: Safety is our top priority. We use professional-grade equipment, have experienced coaches present, and enforce strict rules to ensure a safe environment for everyone.

Q: Can I book a private event at your Montclair location?

A: Yes, we host private events for groups of all sizes. From corporate team-building activities to birthday parties, we can customize the experience to suit your needs.

Q: What is the minimum age for participants?

A: While we welcome visitors of all ages, participants must be at least 12 years old to participate without an adult. Children aged 8-11 can participate with an accompanying adult.
