Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of vein problems do you treat at your Rochester Hills location?

We treat a variety of venous conditions, including varicose veins, spider veins, chronic venous insufficiency (CVI), venous reflux disease, and telangiectasias. If you have concerns about your vein health, we recommend scheduling a consultation for a proper evaluation.

Q2: Do you offer minimally invasive procedures?

Yes, absolutely. Our Rochester Hills center specializes in minimally invasive treatments such as Endovenous Laser Treatment (EVLT), Radiofrequency Ablation (RFA), Venaseal, and sclerotherapy. These procedures typically result in smaller incisions, less pain, minimal scarring, and faster recovery times compared to traditional surgery.

Q3: How long is the recovery time after vein treatment at your clinic?

Recovery times vary depending on the specific procedure performed and individual factors. Generally, patients experience mild discomfort and can return to light activities within a day or two. We provide detailed post-operative instructions during your consultation to ensure a smooth recovery tailored to your needs.

Q4: Is vein treatment covered by insurance?

Most medically necessary vein treatments are covered by insurance. Cosmetic procedures may be subject to different coverage rules. During your initial consultation at our Rochester Hills center, we will review your specific situation and help determine your coverage options.

Q5: How soon can I schedule a consultation at your Rochester Hills location?

We strive to accommodate new patient consultations promptly. You can schedule your appointment by calling our office at 248-629-1346 or by requesting one online through our website: https://www.metroveincenters.com/locations/michigan/rochester-hills.
