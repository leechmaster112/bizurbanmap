Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Botox?A: Botox is a purified form of Botulinum Toxin Type A, a neurotoxin that, when injected in small, localized amounts by a medical professional, temporarily reduces muscle activity in the treated areas, diminishing the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles.
Q: How long does a Botox treatment take?A: The actual Botox injection procedure itself typically takes only 10-20 minutes. You will also spend a few moments in our comfortable Henderson spa for consultation and post-treatment instructions.
Q: How much does Botox cost at Botox La Belle Peau?A: Pricing for Botox treatments varies based on the number of units required and the specific areas being treated. We offer personalized consultations at our Henderson location to determine the ideal treatment plan and provide a customized quote.
Q: Is Botox safe?A: Botox is FDA-approved and has been used safely for many years when administered by qualified professionals. At Botox La Belle Peau in Henderson, our practitioners are highly trained and experienced to ensure your safety and satisfaction.
Q: How long do Botox results last?A: Botox results gradually appear within a few days and reach their full effect within about two weeks. The results typically last 3-6 months, with many clients finding the duration increasing with consistent treatment over time. Regular touch-up appointments are recommended to maintain your desired look.
