Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is IV therapy?

Intravenous (IV) therapy is a medical treatment where nutrients, vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants are administered directly into your bloodstream via an IV drip. This method bypasses the digestive system, ensuring 100% absorption and immediate effects.

How long does an IV therapy session take?

A typical IV therapy session lasts anywhere from 30 minutes to 1.5 hours, depending on the specific treatment, the concentration of the solution, and your individual tolerance. Most sessions are scheduled for about 45-60 minutes.

Is IV therapy safe?

Yes, IV therapy is generally safe when administered by trained professionals using sterile equipment. At Infinite Dripz, we prioritize safety and follow strict hygiene protocols. As with any medical procedure, there may be minor risks like vein irritation or infection at the insertion site, but serious complications are rare.

What conditions can IV therapy help with?

IV therapy can support various conditions and goals, including boosting energy levels, enhancing athletic recovery, strengthening the immune system, alleviating symptoms of chronic fatigue, aiding detoxification, and improving skin health and appearance.

Are there any contraindications for IV therapy?

While generally safe, IV therapy isn't suitable for everyone. Certain medical conditions, such as heart problems, kidney disease, or bleeding disorders, may require caution or preclude treatment. It's crucial to disclose your full medical history during your consultation with our staff at Infinite Dripz.
