USA Ink

USA Ink is a full-service custom apparel and screen printing company based in Arlington, TX, serving the entire Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex for over 20 years. We specialize in screen printing, embroidery, DTG, hybrid printing, and specialty techniques including puff print, metallic foil, glitter, and discharge printing — offering an unmatched range of custom effects under one roof. From single-piece orders to high-volume wholesale and contract printing for other print shops and promotional companies, DFW Ink delivers premium quality with fast, reliable turnaround. Our services include custom t-shirts, polos, hats, beanies, jerseys, sweatshirts, branded uniforms, custom neck tags, and individual garment bagging for brands and resellers. We offer in-house graphic design, free consultations, and flexible ordering — including group order links for individual payments. Local pickup available in Arlington, TX. Trusted by businesses, schools, churches, sports teams, and clothing brands across DFW.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of apparel can USA Ink print on?

A1: USA Ink can print on a wide variety of apparel items, including t-shirts, long-sleeve shirts, hoodies, sweatshirts, tank tops, polos, hats, and more. We work with high-quality garments to ensure your custom prints look great and last.

Q2: How does the custom apparel design process work at USA Ink?

A2: Our design process is collaborative. You can provide your own artwork or logo, or our design team can help you create a custom design from scratch. We'll work with you to ensure the design meets your vision and is optimized for screen printing.

Q3: What is screen printing, and why is it a good option for custom apparel?

A3: Screen printing is a technique where ink is pushed through a stenciled screen onto the fabric. It's an excellent choice for custom apparel because it produces vibrant, durable, and long-lasting prints, especially for designs with fewer colors and for larger order quantities.

Q4: How long does it typically take to receive custom apparel orders from USA Ink?

A4: Turnaround times can vary depending on the complexity of the design, the quantity of the order, and our current production schedule. We always strive to provide an estimated completion time during the quoting process and work diligently to meet your deadlines.

Q5: Can USA Ink handle large bulk orders for businesses or events?

A5: Absolutely! USA Ink is fully equipped to handle large bulk orders for businesses, organizations, events, and teams. Our screen printing process is highly efficient for larger quantities, allowing us to provide competitive pricing and consistent quality for all your apparel needs.
