Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: Do you operate private jets directly from 201 W 5th St?

A1: While 201 W 5th St is our primary operational base for coordinating services and managing client interactions within Austin, actual private jet operations are typically conducted from nearby airports like Austin-Bergstrom International Airport (AUS) or specific FBO locations approved by the client. We manage the entire process, including coordinating ground transportation to and from your downtown address if needed.

Q2: What types of aircraft are available for charter from your Austin base?

A2: We offer a diverse fleet selection suitable for various trip lengths and passenger counts. This includes spacious large-cabin jets for extended business travel, efficient mid-size jets for shorter hops or smaller groups, and versatile turboprops for regional destinations. Our team will consult with you to recommend the most appropriate aircraft for your specific itinerary and requirements.

Q3: How far in advance should I book a private jet flight from Austin?

A3: Booking times depend on your travel needs. For last-minute business travel originating from or returning to the Austin area, we often accommodate requests with as little as 24-48 hours' notice. For leisure travel or more complex itineraries, booking well in advance is recommended to ensure availability and optimal pricing.

Q4: What is included in the price of a private jet charter?

A4: Our quoted prices typically include the base aircraft rental rate, block hours (aircraft time), fuel surcharges, and pilot duties. Depending on the flight, additional charges may apply for landing fees, Air Traffic Control (ATC) fees, FBO services (ground handling, catering, etc.), passenger processing, and potentially customs clearance if flying internationally. We provide transparent pricing and itemize all costs before confirming your booking.

Q5: How does the booking process work for flights departing from Austin?

A5: Our process is straightforward. You can initiate a booking request via our website, phone, or email. Our team will then contact you to gather flight details (dates, times, origin/destination airports, passenger count, special requirements). We will present you with suitable aircraft options and quotes. Once you confirm, we handle flight planning, aircraft preparation, crew coordination, and all necessary documentation. We ensure a seamless experience focused on your convenience from the moment you contact us at 512-596-5978.
