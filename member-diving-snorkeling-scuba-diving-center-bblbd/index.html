Our center combines Swiss precision with Thai hospitality to deliver unforgettable underwater adventures right here in Bophut.

Whether you're a complete beginner eager to explore the underwater world or an experienced diver seeking new challenges, our team of certified instructors is dedicated to ensuring your safety and satisfaction. We offer comprehensive diving courses, guided snorkeling tours, and personalized dive packages tailored to your preferences.

Our facilities are equipped with state-of-the-art equipment and maintained to the highest standards, reflecting our commitment to excellence. From introductory dives to advanced certification, we provide everything you need to embark on your diving journey. Explore the vibrant marine life of Thailand's waters without ever leaving Bophut. At Member Diving, we pride ourselves on creating a welcoming and inclusive environment for all divers. Our center is designed to accommodate both solo travelers and groups, offering flexible booking options and personalized service. Experience the magic of underwater exploration with Member Diving. Our expert team will guide you through every step of the process, from booking to certification. Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Do you offer diving courses for complete beginners?

A: Yes, we provide comprehensive diving courses for absolute beginners. Our courses cover all aspects of scuba diving, from theory to practical skills, and culminate in a recognized certification.

Q2: What equipment do I need to bring for a dive?

A: We provide all necessary diving equipment, including tanks, regulators, and wetsuits. However, if you prefer to use your own gear, you are welcome to bring your personal diving equipment.

Q3: Are there guided snorkeling tours available?

A: Yes, we offer guided snorkeling tours to nearby locations in Bophut. These tours are perfect for those who want to explore the rich marine life without needing extensive diving experience.

Q4: What is the minimum age for diving?

A: The minimum age for diving with us is 10 years old, provided they meet our certification requirements. We also offer specialized junior diving programs for younger participants.

Q5: Do you offer equipment rental for snorkeling?

A: Yes, we provide snorkeling equipment rental, including masks, snorkels, and fins. We also offer guided snorkeling trips to ensure you have the best experience.
