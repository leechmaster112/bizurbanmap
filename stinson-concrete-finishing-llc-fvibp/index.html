We also offer concrete coloring options to add vibrant hues or blend concrete seamlessly with existing surfaces.

Q2: How long does a typical concrete finishing project take from start to finish in Lancaster?

The timeline for a concrete finishing project in Lancaster depends heavily on the specific scope of the work, the condition of the existing concrete, weather conditions (which can significantly impact drying times and curing), and the chosen finishes. This crucial step is performed right here in Lancaster as part of our process.
