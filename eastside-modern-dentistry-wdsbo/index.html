Location

Eastside Modern Dentistry, located at 640 NW Gilman Blvd #100, Issaquah, WA 98027, is dedicated to providing exceptional dental care in a comfortable and modern setting. We understand that visiting the dentist can be a source of anxiety for many, which is why we prioritize patient comfort above all else. Our state-of-the-art facility and compassionate team are committed to making your dental experience positive and stress-free.

At Eastside Modern Dentistry, we offer a comprehensive range of dental services designed to address all your oral health needs. From routine check-ups and cleanings to advanced procedures like dental implants and cosmetic dentistry, we utilize the latest technology and evidence-based techniques to deliver high-quality care. Our focus on modern dentistry ensures that you receive effective, efficient, and minimally invasive treatments tailored to your specific requirements.

Our Services Include:

Dental Examinations and Cleanings: Regular check-ups and professional cleanings to maintain optimal oral health. Filling and Cavity Prevention: Advanced techniques to restore damaged teeth and prevent future decay. Root Canal Therapy: Pain-free and effective treatment to save infected teeth. Crown and Bridge Placement: Durable solutions for restoring damaged or missing teeth. Dental Implants: State-of-the-art options for replacing missing teeth with natural-looking results. Cosmetic Dentistry: Procedures like teeth whitening and veneers to enhance your smile. Oral Surgery: Expert care for complex dental procedures.

We believe that a healthy smile is essential for overall well-being, and we are here to help you achieve and maintain it. Our team is trained to listen to your concerns and provide personalized care, ensuring that you feel informed and comfortable throughout your treatment journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Eastside Modern Dentistry offer?

We provide a wide range of dental services, including preventive care, restorative treatments, cosmetic dentistry, and advanced procedures like dental implants and root canals.

How can I schedule an appointment?

You can schedule an appointment by calling our office at (425) 472-1044 or by booking online through our website.

Does Eastside Modern Dentistry accept insurance?

Yes, we accept most dental insurance plans. Our team will work with you to maximize your benefits and minimize out-of-pocket costs.

We understand that dental emergencies can occur unexpectedly. Please call our office during business hours, and we will do our best to accommodate you. For after-hours emergencies, we can provide you with instructions or direct you to an emergency dental service.
