Act Fast: Your First Steps

In the event of a water emergency, immediate action is vital. Here’s what you should do:

1. Ensure Safety: If the situation is dangerous (electrical risk, structural instability), evacuate the area first. Turn off utilities if safe to do so.

2. Contact Us Immediately: Call 24 HR Water Damage Repair at (888) 450-0858. We prioritize your call and dispatch help promptly.

3. Document the Damage: Take photos or videos for your insurance documentation.

4. Start Wicking: Carefully remove wet personal items from shelves and floors to air dry, if safe to do so.

5. Open Windows/Curtains: If safe, open windows or curtains to improve air circulation.

First, ensure your safety. If there's an electrical hazard or structural risk, evacuate the area. Then, call us immediately at (888) 450-0858. While waiting for our team, you can carefully remove valuables from immediate contact with water and consider documenting the scene with photos or videos.

How quickly can you respond to an emergency?

We understand the urgency of water damage. Our goal is rapid response. While response times can vary slightly based on the severity and time of the emergency, we prioritize dispatching our team as quickly as possible, often within hours of your initial contact, sometimes even sooner.

How do you prevent mold growth after water damage?

Mold prevention is critical. Our process involves rapid water extraction and aggressive drying using specialized equipment. We monitor moisture levels constantly and only declare an area dry when it meets industry standards, significantly reducing the risk of mold development.
