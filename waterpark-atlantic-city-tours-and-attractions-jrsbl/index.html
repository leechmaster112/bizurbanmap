Plan Your Waterpark Day in Atlantic City with One Easy Guide—Passes, Hours, and What to Ride First.

Welcome to Waterpark Atlantic City, your premier destination for fun and excitement right here in Atlantic City, New Jersey. Located at 2701 Pacific Ave, our waterpark offers a refreshing escape from the everyday routine, providing thrilling slides, lazy rivers, and family-friendly attractions all under one roof. We are dedicated to ensuring every visitor has an unforgettable day filled with laughter and adventure.

Planning a visit to our waterpark? We've got you covered. We offer various pass options to suit different needs, ensuring you get the best value for your day at the park. From single-day tickets to season passes, we provide flexibility for visitors looking to enjoy a quick splash or make a day of it. Our passes are designed to be easy to purchase and even easier to use, allowing you to focus on making memories rather than worrying about logistics.

Our hours are designed to accommodate visitors throughout the day, typically opening early in the morning and staying open late into the evening during the peak season. We recommend checking our official website for the most current operating hours, as they may vary slightly depending on the time of year. Staying informed about our hours helps ensure you can plan your perfect day at Waterpark Atlantic City.

When it comes to experiencing our waterpark, the best way to start is often by trying our most popular attractions first. The Summit Plunge, our tallest and fastest water slide, offers an exhilarating rush down a high-speed tube slide. For a more relaxed experience, the lazy river is perfect for floating gently downstream while enjoying the stunning views of the park. Trying these top attractions early in your visit allows you to experience the highlights before exploring the rest of our diverse lineup of rides and amenities.

Waterpark Atlantic City is more than just slides and water activities. We also feature a range of other amenities designed to enhance your day, including a spacious pool area, interactive water play zones for younger guests, and convenient snack and souvenir shops. Our attentive staff is always on hand to assist you, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable visit for everyone in your party.

For detailed information about our passes, hours, and other services, please visit our official website or call our friendly team at (609) 555-2740. We look forward to welcoming you to Waterpark Atlantic City for an amazing day of fun!

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What are the current operating hours for Waterpark Atlantic City?

Our hours vary depending on the season. During peak summer months, we typically open early in the morning and close late in the evening. For the most accurate and up-to-date information, please visit our website or call (609) 555-2740.

Q2: Can I purchase tickets online in advance?

Absolutely! You can purchase single-day passes, season passes, and group tickets online through our website. Buying tickets online often includes special discounts and guarantees entry without long wait times at the gate.

Q3: Are there any age restrictions for the waterpark attractions?

Yes, some attractions have height or age restrictions for safety reasons. Information regarding these restrictions is clearly posted at each ride and during the check-in process. Our staff will be happy to assist you and guide your family to the perfect attractions.

Q4: What payment methods do you accept for tickets and merchandise?

We accept major credit cards, debit cards, and cash at our ticket booth and merchandise shops. Please bring your preferred payment method for a smooth transaction.

Q5: Is there parking available at Waterpark Atlantic City?

Yes, we offer ample parking for visitors. The parking lot is conveniently located near the entrance and is well-lit and secure. Please note that parking fees may apply, and regulations can change, so it's best to confirm details when you arrive or call (609) 555-2740 for more information.
