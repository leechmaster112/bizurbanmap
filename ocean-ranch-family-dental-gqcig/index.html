Ocean Ranch Family Dental

Ocean Ranch Family Dental is a premier dental practice serving Oceanside, Vista, and all of San Diego County, founded in 2013 by Dr. Alek Zand, a distinguished dentist with over 18 years of experience in comprehensive oral healthcare. Located at 3609 Ocean Ranch Blvd STE 205 in Oceanside, CA, Ocean Ranch Family Dental combines advanced dental expertise with a warm, welcoming environment where patients of all ages receive the highest quality care tailored to their individual needs. Dr. Zand, a graduate of the University of Southern California School of Dentistry where he earned the Dean's Award for Clinical Excellence, has built a practice renowned for its emphasis on cutting-edge dental technology and patient-centered care. The practice offers a full spectrum of dental services including preventive care, restorative dentistry, cosmetic enhancements, and emergency treatments, with particular expertise in dental implants, Invisalign orthodontics, root canal therapy, crowns, veneers, dental bridges, teeth whitening, and advanced periodontal treatments including deep cleanings. At Ocean Ranch Family Dental, patients experience compassionate, gentle care delivered by a friendly and experienced team dedicated to promoting lifelong oral health and creating beautiful, confident smiles. The practice is committed to making dental care accessible and convenient, offering flexible scheduling options to accommodate busy lifestyles and accepting most PPO dental insurance plans including Aetna, MetLife, Guardian, Principal, and Delta Dental, along with flexible financing options. Whether patients are experiencing a dental emergency, bringing their child for their first dental visit, needing routine maintenance, or seeking a complete smile makeover, Ocean Ranch Family Dental provides exceptional care using modern techniques designed to ensure comfort and optimal results. Beyond clinical excellence, Dr. Zand and his team are deeply committed to patient education and community service, regularly participating in local health fairs and providing charitable dental care to underserved populations. The practice philosophy centers on prevention and education, with personalized oral hygiene plans developed for each patient to maintain healthy smiles for life.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do you offer emergency dental care?

A: Yes, we understand dental emergencies can happen anytime. Please call our office at (760) 231-5522 during regular business hours, or seek immediate care if it's a true emergency.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?

A: We accept cash, checks, and most major insurance plans. We also offer financing options for qualified patients. Please contact our office for details.

Q: Do you provide preventative care services?

A: Absolutely! We offer comprehensive preventative services including dental exams, cleanings, X-rays, and fluoride treatments to keep your smile healthy.

Q: Can my child receive dental treatment here?

A: Yes, our practice is designed to accommodate patients of all ages, including children. We offer pediatric dental services in a child-friendly environment.

Q: How often should I schedule a dental checkup?

A: We generally recommend scheduling a dental checkup and cleaning every six months for most patients. However, your specific needs may vary.
