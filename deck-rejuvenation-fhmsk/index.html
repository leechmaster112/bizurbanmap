Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) for Deck Rejuvenation in Carlton VIC

1. Q: How long does a typical Deck Rejuvenation service take for a standard deck in Carlton?

A: The duration depends on the deck's size, condition, and the specific services chosen (cleaning, sanding, staining, sealing, or repair). A site assessment is usually required to provide an accurate timeframe, but generally, a standard deck can be transformed in a single day or over a few days, depending on the extent of work and weather conditions.

2. Q: What types of wood decks does Deck Rejuvenation work on in Carlton?

A: Deck Rejuvenation is experienced with a variety of timber types commonly found in Carlton, including but not limited to hardwoods like Ipe, Spotted Gum, Jarrah, and softer timbers like Pine and Cedar. Our techniques are adaptable to suit different wood species.

3. Q: Do you offer a warranty on your deck rejuvenation services performed in Carlton?

A: Yes, we stand behind the quality of our workmanship and the products we use. Deck Rejuvenation typically offers a warranty on our services, covering issues like peeling stain or sealant failure for a specified period (often 1-2 years), subject to terms and conditions, including proper maintenance.

4. Q: How often should I have my deck professionally rejuvenated if it's in Carlton?

A: This depends on the type of wood, the quality of the initial installation, the climate exposure (sun, rain), and how much foot traffic it receives. Generally, a professional deep clean and recoat every 2-5 years is recommended to maintain both the appearance and protection of your deck. Regular maintenance between professional services can help extend the intervals.

5. Q: Can Deck Rejuvenation handle staining and sealing on the same day?

A: Often yes, especially for smaller decks or if the existing deck condition allows it. However, factors like weather (temperature, humidity, rain forecast) and the specific products used can influence this. Our team will advise during the assessment whether staining and sealing can be completed in a single visit or if separate sessions are preferable for optimal results.
