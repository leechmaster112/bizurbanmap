Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs):

What services do you offer?

We primarily offer professional pressure washing and soft washing services. Pressure washing is ideal for hard, durable surfaces like concrete, stone, and brick, using high-pressure water to remove dirt and grime. Soft washing is perfect for more delicate surfaces such as vinyl siding, wood decks, composite materials, landscaping features, and awnings, using lower pressure and specialized cleaning solutions to avoid damage.

Do you clean residential and commercial properties?

Absolutely! Pristine Clean Exteriors proudly serves both residential and commercial clients in the Rogers, AR area. Whether you're a homeowner looking to refresh your home's exterior or a business owner wanting to maintain a professional image, we have the expertise and equipment to meet your needs.

How does the pressure washing process work?

Our pressure washing process typically involves several steps: a thorough inspection of the surface, application of appropriate cleaning solutions (if needed), high-pressure hot or cold water washing to remove dirt and debris, and a final rinse. We adjust pressure and techniques based on the surface material to ensure safety and optimal results.

How does the soft washing process differ?

Soft washing uses lower water pressure and focuses on surface preparation and the application of specialized cleaning agents tailored to the specific type of surface and stain. We often use scrubbing brushes or rotary brushes to agitate the surface and loosen dirt before rinsing thoroughly with lower pressure water. This method is gentler and prevents damage to delicate materials.

Are your services affordable?

We strive to provide high-quality exterior cleaning services at competitive and affordable prices. We believe in fair pricing based on the size of the job, the surfaces involved, and the level of cleaning required. You can learn more about our pricing by contacting us or visiting our website.
