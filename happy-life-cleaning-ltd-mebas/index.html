Frequently Asked Questions

1. What cleaning services do you offer?

We provide a range of house cleaning services, including standard cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in/move-out cleaning, kitchen and bathroom deep cleans, and regular maintenance cleaning for homes in the Calgary area.

2. How far in advance should I book a cleaning?

We recommend booking your cleaning service at least 24-48 hours in advance to ensure availability and allow us to accommodate your schedule. For larger or more complex jobs, booking ahead is appreciated.

3. How long does a typical cleaning take?

The duration of a cleaning depends on the size of your home and the scope of the service. A standard one-bedroom apartment might take 1-2 hours, while a larger home could require 3-5 hours or more. We will provide a specific estimate based on your needs.

4. Do you provide cleaning supplies?

Yes, we bring all necessary cleaning supplies, equipment, and eco-friendly products to ensure a thorough clean without requiring anything from you.

5. What should I do to prepare for a cleaning?

To ensure a smooth and efficient cleaning process, please take out the trash, clear countertops, and put items back in their designated spots if possible. This allows us to focus on a deep clean and makes the process quicker.
