Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of Remy hair extensions do you offer at your Bristol salon?

We offer a variety of premium Remy hair extension methods, including tape-ins, micro-beads, micro-loop fusion, and seamless tape integration. Our team can advise on the best option for your hair type, desired look, and lifestyle during your consultation.

Q2: How long does a typical hair extension application take at Flawless Hair Extensions Bristol?

The time required for an extension application varies depending on the number of extensions and the chosen method. Generally, a full head application can take between 3 to 5 hours. We recommend scheduling ample time for your appointment.

Q3: Do your Remy hair extensions look and feel completely natural?

Absolutely. Our Remy hair extensions are cut from a single donor hair, preserving the cuticle layer for a smooth, tangle-free appearance and natural movement. When installed correctly by our expert stylists, they blend seamlessly with your natural hair.

Q4: How should I care for my Remy hair extensions after getting them installed?

Proper care is essential for maintaining the beauty and longevity of your extensions. We provide detailed aftercare instructions during your visit, which typically include gentle shampooing, conditioning, detangling, and avoiding excessive heat styling. Following these guidelines will help your extensions look their best.

Q5: Can I bring in my own Remy hair extensions for installation at your Bristol location?

Yes, we can install high-quality Remy hair extensions that you have purchased elsewhere. Please ensure they are clean and confirm they are genuine Remy hair. Our team will assess them before proceeding with the application.
