Q3: How often should I schedule a skin check-up at Hello Skin MD in Phoenix?

A: We recommend scheduling a baseline skin examination with us at our Phoenix location, especially if you have significant sun exposure history or numerous moles. For most patients, an annual skin check-up is sufficient for preventative care. However, individuals with a history of skin cancer or precancerous lesions may require more frequent evaluations.
