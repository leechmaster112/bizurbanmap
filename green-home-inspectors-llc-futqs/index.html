Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What does a standard home inspection include?

A standard home inspection conducted by Green Home Inspectors LLC typically includes an assessment of the property's structure, roof, exterior, interior, plumbing, electrical systems, HVAC, insulation, and more. Our detailed report will highlight the condition of these elements and point out any areas of concern.

2. How long does a home inspection take?

Typically, a standard home inspection takes between 2 to 3 hours, depending on the size and age of the property. Our thorough approach ensures that we cover every critical area without rushing.

3. When should I schedule a home inspection?

It is recommended to schedule a home inspection after you have made an offer on a property but before you finalize the purchase. This allows you to address any concerns or negotiate repairs based on the findings.

4. What happens if the inspection reveals problems?

If the inspection uncovers issues, our detailed report will outline them clearly. This information empowers you to make informed decisions, whether it's negotiating with the seller, requesting repairs, or deciding to proceed with the purchase as is.

5. Do you provide follow-up services?

Yes, Green Home Inspectors LLC offers follow-up services to monitor the condition of your property over time. We can help you track any changes or potential issues that may arise, ensuring your home remains in optimal condition.
