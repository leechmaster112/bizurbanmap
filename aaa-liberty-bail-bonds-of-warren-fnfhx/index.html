Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is a bail bond?

A bail bond is a contract whereby a bail bondsman guarantees to the court that a defendant will appear for all required court proceedings. In exchange for this guarantee, the defendant or their family pays a non-refundable fee (usually around 10% of the total bail amount) to the bondsman.

Q4: What happens if the defendant doesn't show up for court?

If the defendant fails to appear in court, the bail bond is considered forfeited. The bail bond fee paid to AAA Liberty Bail Bonds of Warren is typically not refundable in this situation.
