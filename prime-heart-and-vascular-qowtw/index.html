Our Vascular Services

Our vascular specialists focus on the health of your blood vessels, including arteries and veins. We diagnose and treat conditions affecting these vessels, which are crucial for delivering oxygen and nutrients throughout your body. Common vascular issues addressed at our Allen location include peripheral artery disease, carotid artery disease, coronary artery disease, aneurysms, venous reflux disease, and varicose veins.

We offer a range of diagnostic services, such as vascular ultrasounds and ankle-brachial index tests, to accurately identify vascular problems. Treatment options may include medication, minimally invasive procedures like angioplasty and stenting, or surgical interventions, all managed within our dedicated vascular care facility in Allen.
