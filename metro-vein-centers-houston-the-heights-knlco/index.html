Our services include procedures like sclerotherapy, endovenous laser treatment (EVLT), and radiofrequency ablation, tailored to the specific type and severity of veins you may have.

Q: Are the treatments painful?A: While some discomfort may be felt during the procedure, our team uses local anesthesia and advanced techniques to ensure your comfort. Most patients report minimal pain and describe the recovery as manageable.

Q: How much does treatment typically cost?A: Costs vary depending on the complexity of your veins, the chosen treatment method, and insurance coverage. We recommend scheduling a consultation at our Heights location for a personalized estimate.

Q: How long is the recovery period?A: Recovery is generally quick. You may resume light activities immediately after your appointment, but we advise avoiding strenuous exercise for a few days. Compression stockings are usually required for a specified period post-treatment.

Q: Are the results permanent?A: Treatments significantly reduce the appearance and symptoms of varicose and spider veins. While new veins can develop over time, existing treated veins typically do not return.
