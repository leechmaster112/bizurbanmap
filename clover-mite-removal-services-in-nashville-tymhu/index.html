Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What exactly are clover mites?

A: Clover mites are tiny, reddish-brown arachnids, often found clustered together. They are not true spiders or ticks but are related. They are commonly mistaken for dust but can be seen as distinct specks, especially when crushed or disturbed.

Q2: How do I know if I have a clover mite problem?

A: You might notice tiny reddish-brown specks on window sills, walls, or curtains, especially after windy days or rain. Sometimes, crushing them can leave a dark stain. A professional inspection is the best way to confirm an infestation at your property.

Q3: Are clover mites dangerous?

A: While clover mites do not bite or transmit diseases to humans or pets, their large populations can cause damage to window sills and frames by excreting corrosive waste. Their main impact is being a nuisance pest.
