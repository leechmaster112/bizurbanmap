Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How often should I get my septic tank pumped?

A: Generally, most septic tanks require pumping every 3 to 5 years. However, the exact frequency depends on factors like tank size, number of people in the household, water usage, and the amount of solids entering the system. Regular inspections are recommended to determine the optimal pumping schedule for your specific situation.

Q2: What is included in a standard septic tank pumping service?

A: Our standard septic tank pumping service includes arriving at your property, locating the tank, pumping out all the accumulated sludge and scum, disposing of the waste according to regulations, and providing you with a receipt for the service. We also perform a visual inspection of the tank and surrounding area during the pumping process.

Q3: What types of drain lines do you service?

A: We service all types of drain lines found in residential and commercial properties in the Perry area. This includes indoor kitchen and bathroom drains, outdoor drainage systems, main sewer lines, and both interior and exterior septic field lines.

Q4: Do you offer emergency services?

A: Yes, we understand plumbing and septic emergencies can happen anytime. We offer emergency service calls for urgent issues like major drain backups or potential septic system failures. Please call our phone number immediately for prompt assistance.

Q5: How far in advance should I schedule my septic pumping?

A: We recommend scheduling your septic tank pumping well in advance, ideally 2-4 weeks before you anticipate needing the service. This ensures availability and allows us to schedule the most convenient time for you, minimizing any potential disruption to your routine.
