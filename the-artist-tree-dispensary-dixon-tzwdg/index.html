The Artist Tree Dispensary Dixon

The Artist Tree Dixon is a licensed cannabis dispensary serving Dixon, CA and Solano County. The store offers a curated selection of cannabis flower, pre-rolls, vapes, edibles, concentrates, and topicals. The space features an upscale, gallery-style layout with rotating artwork by local artists. In-store shopping and pickup are available. Their friendly knowledgeable staff focuses on education and efficiency.
