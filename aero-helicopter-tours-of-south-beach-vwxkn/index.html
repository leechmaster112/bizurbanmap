Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: Where do the helicopter tours depart from?

A: All Aero Helicopter Tours depart directly from our base located at 1626 Collins Ave, Miami Beach, FL 33139.

Q2: How long does a typical sightseeing tour last?

A: Our standard sightseeing tour typically lasts approximately 15-20 minutes, providing a perfect balance of flight time and scenic views over Miami Beach.

Q3: What is included in the tour price?

A: The tour price includes the helicopter flight itself, the professional pilot, and all safety equipment. We recommend booking in advance, especially for special occasions.

Q4: Are the helicopters safe?

A: Absolutely. Aero Helicopter Tours adheres strictly to all FAA regulations and safety standards. Our helicopters undergo regular maintenance, and our pilots are highly experienced and certified professionals dedicated to your safety.

Q5: Can I take photos or videos during the flight?

A: Yes, you are welcome to take personal photos and videos during the flight. We also offer professional aerial photography and videography services for an additional fee, perfect for capturing memories of your Miami Beach adventure.
