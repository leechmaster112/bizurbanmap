Welcome to Lucky Detroit Coffee Roasters Espresso Grosse Pointe, your premier destination for exceptional coffee and espresso experiences right here in Grosse Pointe. Our skilled roasters carefully select and roast each batch to bring out unique flavors and aromas that delight the senses. Whether you're a dedicated espresso connoisseur or simply enjoy a perfectly brewed cup of coffee, we have something special waiting for you.

We offer a wide range of coffee drinks, from classic favorites like lattes and cappuccinos to innovative creations crafted by our talented baristas. Our espresso program is at the heart of what we do, featuring freshly pulled shots that form the base for many of our popular beverages.

Our beans are roasted in small batches to ensure maximum freshness and flavor. We believe in transparency, so you can learn about our roasting process and the origins of the beans we use.
