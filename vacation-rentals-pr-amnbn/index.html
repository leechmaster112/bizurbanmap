Vacation Rentals PR

Puerto Rico Property offers spacious and fully furnished vacation rentals in Puerto Rico, specializing in large homes for families, groups, events, and travelers looking to experience the island in comfort. Our properties are located in popular areas including San Juan, Carolina, and Bayamón, giving guests convenient access to the island’s best beaches, nightlife, shopping, and attractions. Our vacation homes feature 5- to 18-bedroom rentals, making them ideal for family vacations, group trips, corporate retreats, and special events. Many of our properties are located near popular destinations such as Condado, Old San Juan, and El Yunque National Forest. Guests can enjoy nearby beaches, restaurants, casinos, nightlife at La Placita, outdoor adventures, rainforest tours, jet skiing, horseback riding, and more. Each property is designed to provide a comfortable and memorable stay with amenities such as multiple bedrooms, spacious living areas, full kitchens, pools, large yards, BBQ areas, and convenient parking. Many homes are centrally located just minutes from the airport, beaches, shopping centers, and Puerto Rico’s most famous attractions. Whether you're planning a group vacation in San Juan, a family getaway near the beach, or a large vacation rental in Puerto Rico, our goal is to make the booking process simple and your stay unforgettable. Our host Steve provides personalized assistance and can help guests plan their stay in English, Spanish, Italian, and Portuguese. Discover the beauty of Puerto Rico with comfortable accommodations, prime locations, and exceptional hospitality.
