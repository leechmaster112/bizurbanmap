What is TMS therapy and how can it help me?

TMS is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain. It is an FDA-cleared treatment for Major Depressive Disorder, particularly for individuals who have not experienced satisfactory improvement from prior antidepressant medication. It is a safe and effective alternative offered at our Riverview clinic.
