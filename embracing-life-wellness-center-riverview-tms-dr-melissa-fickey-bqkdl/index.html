Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is TMS therapy and how is it performed at your Riverview clinic?

TMS, or Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation, is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain to improve symptoms of depression. At our clinic at 6332 US-301, Riverview, FL 33578, patients relax in a comfortable chair while a magnetic coil is gently placed against their scalp. The treatment is administered by our trained staff under the supervision of Dr. Melissa Fickey, and patients can return to their daily activities immediately afterward.

2. Do I need a referral to be seen at Embracing Life Wellness Center?

While a referral from your primary care physician or another healthcare provider can be helpful, it is not a requirement to schedule an appointment. You can contact our Riverview office directly at (813) 662-5919 to schedule a consultation with Dr. Melissa Fickey to discuss your needs and determine the best course of treatment.

3. What conditions do you treat at your location on US-301?

Our primary focus at this location is on providing TMS therapy for Major Depressive Disorder. We also offer comprehensive psychiatric services, including diagnosis, medication management, and psychotherapy for a range of mental health conditions. A consultation will help us understand your specific situation and how we can best assist you.

4. How do I know if TMS therapy is right for me?

TMS therapy is typically recommended for adults who have not experienced satisfactory improvement from prior antidepressant medication. The best way to determine if you are a candidate is to schedule a consultation with Dr. Melissa Fickey at our Riverview clinic. She will conduct a thorough assessment of your history and symptoms to see if TMS is an appropriate treatment option for you.
