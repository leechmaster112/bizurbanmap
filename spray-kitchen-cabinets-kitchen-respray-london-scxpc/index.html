Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: How long does a typical kitchen cabinet respray project take?

Our process is efficient, but the timeline depends on the size of your kitchen and the number of cabinets being resprayed. Generally, you can expect the project to be completed within a few days to a week, allowing minimal disruption to your home life.

Q2: What types of cabinet materials can you spray?

We specialise in spraying a variety of materials, including laminated, melamine, MDF, and painted wood surfaces commonly found in kitchens. Our expertise ensures we handle different textures effectively.

Q3: Do you handle the preparation of the cabinets before spraying?

Absolutely. Thorough preparation is crucial for a durable and attractive finish. We meticulously clean, sand, and touch up any minor damages to ensure the paint adheres perfectly and the final result is flawless.

Q4: Can I choose custom colours for my kitchen respray?

Yes, we offer a wide range of standard colours and can source custom colours to match your specific requirements. Our colour matching service ensures the new finish complements your existing kitchen décor seamlessly.

Q5: What kind of warranty do you offer on your respray work?

We stand behind the quality of our workmanship and provide a warranty on the spray finish, typically lasting several years. This reflects our confidence in the durability and professionalism of our service.
