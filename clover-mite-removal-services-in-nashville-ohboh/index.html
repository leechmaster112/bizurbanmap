Q1: What exactly are "tiny red bugs," and how do I know if I have them?

These tiny red bugs are commonly referred to as clover mites. They are often mistaken for dust or spider webs but can be seen clustering on window sills, walls, or curtains, especially after rain or in sunny conditions. They are typically reddish-brown or red in color and very small. If you notice these tiny red specks moving collectively, or if family members are experiencing unusual itching or irritation, especially near windows or walls, you may have a clover mite infestation.
