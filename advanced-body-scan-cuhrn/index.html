Our Services in Bellaire

At our Bellaire location, we offer a range of essential preventive scan services:

Heart Scan (Coronary Artery Calcium - CAC) Scan: This non-invasive, painless procedure uses state-of-the-art CT technology to measure the amount of calcium buildup in your coronary arteries. The presence of calcium is a strong indicator of atherosclerosis, a condition linked to an increased risk of heart attack and stroke. The CAC score provides valuable information about your heart health and helps determine your risk level for cardiovascular disease.

Lung Scan: Our advanced lung imaging services help detect early signs of lung conditions, including lung cancer and chronic lung diseases. These scans are crucial for early diagnosis and effective treatment planning.

Body Scan: Our comprehensive body scans offer a detailed look at various internal structures. Depending on the specific needs, these scans can focus on different areas of the body, providing valuable insights into your overall health.

Virtual Colonoscopy (CT Colonography): This innovative, non-invasive alternative to traditional colonoscopy uses CT technology to create detailed images of the colon and rectum. It's an effective screening tool for colorectal cancer, offering comfort and convenience while maintaining high diagnostic accuracy.
