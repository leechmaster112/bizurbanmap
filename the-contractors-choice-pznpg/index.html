From project inception to final walkthrough, contractors face potential hazards including equipment breakdown, third-party property damage, bodily injury, vehicle accidents involving job sites, and the complexities of bonding and project financing. Operating successfully and safely requires robust protection specifically designed for the construction industry. At The Contractor's Choice Agency, we specialize in navigating these complexities.

Our Core Insurance Services for Local Contractors

We offer a comprehensive suite of insurance products meticulously crafted for contractors working in the Phoenix area:

General Liability Insurance

This is fundamental coverage for any contractor. It protects your business from claims of bodily injury, property damage, personal injury (like libel or slander), and advertising injury occurring on your job sites or due to your business operations within the Phoenix vicinity. Should a client or individual claim injury due to your work on a project in Maricopa County, this policy helps cover the costs.

Commercial Property Insurance

Your tools, equipment, vehicles, and even your place of business (if applicable locally) are valuable assets. This insurance protects against financial loss resulting from damage to these physical assets caused by events like fire, windstorms, vandalism, or other perils, ensuring you can recover and continue operations from our Phoenix location.

Workers' Compensation Insurance

Protecting your valued employees is paramount. Workers' compensation insurance provides crucial coverage for medical expenses, lost wages, and rehabilitation costs if an employee is injured on the job, whether it's on a residential, commercial, or industrial project site within Phoenix. This coverage is often mandated by state law.

Commercial Auto Insurance

Contractors rely heavily on vehicles for transporting materials, equipment, and personnel. This specialized auto insurance covers liability and damage costs associated with company-owned or rented vehicles used for business operations, including accidents involving job sites or deliveries within the Phoenix area.

Builder's Risk Insurance

Protecting the structure being built is critical. Builder's risk insurance covers damage to a construction project before it's completed. This can include damage from fire, vandalism, weather events, or other causes specific to the building process, safeguarding your investment on projects happening right here in Phoenix.

Commercial Bonding

Performance and payment bonds are often required by clients and regulatory bodies for larger contracts. These bonds guarantee that you will fulfill your contractual obligations (performance bond) and pay subcontractors, suppliers, and laborers (payment bond). We assist contractors in securing the necessary bonds to bid on and successfully complete projects within the local Phoenix market.

Business Owner's Policy (BOP)

For many contractors, a BOP offers a convenient and cost-effective package combining essential coverages like General Liability and Property Insurance into one streamlined policy, tailored specifically for small to medium-sized construction businesses operating locally.
