Q: Do I have a valid claim if I was partially at fault for my Shelby Township car accident?

A: Michigan follows a comparative negligence system. You may still be entitled to recover damages even if you were partially at fault, as long as your share of fault is less than 50%. We will carefully analyze the specific circumstances of your Shelby Township accident to determine liability and your potential recovery.
