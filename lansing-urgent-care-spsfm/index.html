Lansing Urgent Care

We created Lansing Urgent Care from a desire to offer you a refreshing solution to an often inefficient, overpriced and stressful healthcare system. Though your primary care physician is always your best first contact for your health needs, primary care physicians are often overburdened which leads to appointment delays at the moment you need care. The emergency room is an over utilized, expensive and stressful place to seek healthcare when you don’t have a true medical emergency. In fact, the cost of care received at the ER is approximately 700% higher than similar care received at an urgent care! We offer a solution.
