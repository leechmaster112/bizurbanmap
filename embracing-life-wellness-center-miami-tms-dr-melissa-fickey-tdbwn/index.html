Frequently Asked Questions

What is TMS therapy and how is it performed at your Coral Gables office?

TMS therapy is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic pulses to stimulate areas of the brain involved in mood regulation. At our office at 2600 Douglas Rd Suite 711, you will be seated in a comfortable chair. A magnetic coil is gently placed against your head, and you will hear a clicking sound and feel a light tapping sensation during the treatment, which lasts about 20-40 minutes. You remain awake and can return to your daily activities immediately afterward.

Who is a candidate for TMS treatment at your clinic?

Ideal candidates for TMS at our Coral Gables location are adults who have been diagnosed with Major Depressive Disorder and have not experienced satisfactory improvement from previous antidepressant medications. A consultation with Dr. Melissa Fickey at our office is necessary to conduct a full assessment and determine if TMS is the right treatment for you.

What can I expect during my first visit to your wellness center?

Your first visit to our clinic at 2600 Douglas Rd Suite 711 involves a comprehensive evaluation with Dr. Melissa Fickey. This includes a detailed review of your mental health history, current symptoms, and previous treatments. This appointment is crucial for developing a personalized understanding of your needs and discussing whether TMS or another service we offer is the most appropriate option.

Are the services provided at your specific location covered by insurance?

Many insurance plans do provide coverage for TMS therapy. Our administrative team at the Coral Gables office will work with you to verify your specific insurance benefits and outline any out-of-pocket costs before you begin your treatment plan. We are committed to providing clarity regarding the financial aspect of your care.
