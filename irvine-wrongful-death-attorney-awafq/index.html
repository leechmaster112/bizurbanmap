Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is considered a wrongful death in Irvine?

A wrongful death occurs in Irvine when the negligent or intentional actions of another party cause the death of a person. Common examples include car accidents, workplace accidents, medical malpractice, and dangerous property conditions.

Q: How long do I have to file a wrongful death claim in Irvine?

In Irvine, the statute of limitations for filing a wrongful death claim is typically two years from the date of the deceased person's death. It's crucial to consult with an attorney promptly.

Q: Do I need a wrongful death attorney for my Irvine case?

Yes, hiring an Irvine wrongful death attorney is highly recommended. They possess the local expertise, resources, and understanding of California law to effectively navigate your case and fight for maximum compensation.

Q: What types of compensation can I seek in an Irvine wrongful death case?

Compensation may include funeral and burial expenses, medical bills, lost income, loss of companionship and support, and pain and suffering for the surviving family members in Irvine.

Q: How much does it cost to hire an Irvine wrongful death attorney?

Most Irvine wrongful death attorneys, including ours, work on a contingency fee basis. This means you only pay if we win your case, and our fee is a percentage of the settlement or court award.
