Navesink Wellness Center

Navesink Wellness Center (NWC) is a premier mental health and wellness collective in Lincroft, NJ, dedicated to reimagining the therapeutic experience for women, children, couples and families in Monmouth County. What sets NWC apart is our commitment to "Continuous Connection." We recognize that the most challenging moments happen outside the therapy room. To bridge this gap, we provide our clients with the Mindstream Wellness app, a proprietary tool that allows for real-time journaling and clinician feedback, ensuring support is proactive and helps our clients get to the root of the issues in order to make lasting change. With our team of renowned clinicians, NWC offers a modern, connected model of care designed for the way you live.
