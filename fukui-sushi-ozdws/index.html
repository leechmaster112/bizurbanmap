Location

Fukui Sushi

Experience the exquisite art of sushi and authentic Japanese cuisine at Fukui Sushi, located at 1612 Bayview Ave in the heart of Toronto. We take pride in offering a dining experience that transports you straight to the essence of Japan, with every dish prepared with the utmost care, precision, and the freshest ingredients.

Our menu boasts a wide selection of traditional Japanese dishes, carefully curated to satisfy both the seasoned sushi connoisseur and the curious newcomer. Indulge in our signature Omakase experience, where our skilled sushi master presents a curated selection of premium pieces, often including limited-edition cuts, served in perfect harmony. For those seeking a more structured meal, our standard menu features a delightful array of nigiri, sashimi, rolls, and other classics, all prepared according to authentic recipes.

Step into our welcoming and meticulously clean dining room, designed to provide a serene atmosphere that complements the culinary journey ahead. We believe that the perfect ambiance is essential to fully appreciate the quality of our food. Whether you're celebrating a special occasion, enjoying a casual dinner, or simply craving the finest sushi in the vicinity, Fukui Sushi at 1612 Bayview Ave is your destination.

Discover the difference that dedication to tradition and quality makes. Visit our website to view our full menu, learn more about our chef, and plan your visit. For reservations, please call us directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What type of sushi does Fukui Sushi offer?

A1: Fukui Sushi offers a comprehensive selection of authentic Japanese sushi and sashimi, including traditional nigiri, temaki rolls, and various other preparations. Our focus is on using the freshest ingredients available.

Q2: Do you offer an Omakase experience?

A2: Yes, we offer an Omakase experience at our location on Bayview Ave. This is a chef's choice selection of premium sushi and Japanese delicacies, served in a specific order.

Q3: What are your typical business hours?

A3: Our hours may vary depending on the day of the week. We recommend calling our location directly at (416) 487-3388 or checking our website for the most current information on opening times.

Q4: Can I make a reservation for a group?

A4: Yes, we welcome groups of all sizes. For parties larger than a certain number, reservations are recommended. You can book your table by calling (416) 487-3388 or using our online reservation system on our website.

Q5: Are there vegetarian or vegan options available?

A5: Yes, we offer vegetarian and vegan options. Please inform your server of your dietary requirements when placing your order. While some traditional rolls contain fish, we can often accommodate requests for plant-based ingredients.
