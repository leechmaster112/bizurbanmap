Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What exactly is Paint Protection Film (PPF)?

A1: Paint Protection Film is a clear, durable film applied to your car's front bumper and other vulnerable areas. It shields the original paint from scratches, chips, rock chips, bug splatter, and environmental damage. It often features a self-healing property that repairs minor scratches.

Q2: How long does the PPF application process take?

A2: The time required for a full PPF application can vary depending on the vehicle size and complexity, but typically ranges from one to three days. This allows our technicians at our Syracuse location ample time to ensure a precise and flawless application.

Q3: What is Ceramic Coating and how does it benefit my car?

A3: Ceramic coating is a liquid polymer that bonds with your paint to create a hydrophobic (water-repelling) and oleophobic (oil-repelling) barrier. Benefits include enhanced gloss and depth, easier cleaning, protection against UV damage and environmental contaminants, and increased durability for your paint and any PPF applied underneath.

Q4: Can I get Ceramic Coating applied over my existing paint without PPF?

A4: Yes, absolutely! Our ceramic coating service is designed to be applied directly over your vehicle's original factory paint. It enhances the appearance and provides a protective layer.

Q5: How long does the protection from PPF and Ceramic Coating last?

A5: With proper care and maintenance, high-quality PPF can last for 5-10 years or more. Ceramic coating typically lasts 2-5 years, depending on the specific product used and how well it's maintained. We recommend regular washes and occasional reapplications to maintain the Ceramic Coat's effectiveness.
