Frequently Asked Questions

1. What are the typical operating hours at Oakwood Bar and Grill?

The Grill is open for lunch from 11:00 AM to 2:00 PM and dinner from 4:30 PM to 9:30 PM, Monday through Sunday. Please call (313) 451-8970 for the most current hours.

2. Do you offer catering services for events in the Dearborn area?

Yes, Oakwood Bar and Grill provides catering services for private events held locally. For more information or to place a catering order, please contact the restaurant directly using the provided phone number.

3. Is reservations required for lunch or dinner at Oakwood Bar and Grill?

While walk-ins are welcome, reservations are highly recommended, especially for parties of six or more, or during peak dining times. You can make reservations by calling (313) 451-8970.

4. What types of alcoholic beverages are available at Oakwood Bar and Grill?

The bar offers a wide selection of handcrafted cocktails, fine wines by the glass, domestic and imported beers, and a rotating list of craft cocktails. Full bar service is available during dinner hours.

5. Is there a parking lot available for customers dining at Oakwood Bar and Grill?

Yes, Oakwood Bar and Grill provides a dedicated parking lot for its guests. Please note that parking spaces are limited, and valet service is not currently offered.
