Location

Welcome to Dr. Kelly's Surgical Unit - North Phoenix, located at 4727 E Bell Rd #11, Phoenix, AZ 85032. We are a specialized veterinary practice dedicated exclusively to providing high-quality surgical care for your beloved pets right here in our North Phoenix community.

At Dr. Kelly's Surgical Unit, we understand that bringing your pet to a surgical facility can be a stressful time for both you and your furry family member. Our primary focus is to ensure that your experience, from the initial consultation through recovery, is as smooth and stress-free as possible. We pride ourselves on delivering compassionate, expert surgical care tailored specifically to the needs of the pets and their owners who come to us from the vicinity of our Phoenix location.

Our state-of-the-art surgical facility is designed with your pet's safety and comfort as our top priorities. We utilize the latest advancements in veterinary surgical technology and adhere strictly to the highest standards of hygiene and infection control protocols. This commitment allows us to perform a wide range of surgical procedures with precision, minimizing discomfort and promoting faster recovery times for your pets.

Our Specialized Services at Dr. Kelly's Surgical Unit include:

Spay and Neuter Surgeries: Essential procedures for population control and preventing serious health issues. Dental Surgery: Including tooth extractions and oral tumor removals to improve dental health and overall well-being. Soft Tissue Surgery: Addressing conditions such as foreign body removal, wound repair, tumor removal, and internal mass biopsies. Orthopedic Surgery: Treating fractures, ligament repairs (like cruciate ligament), and addressing other musculoskeletal issues. Mass Removal: Excising benign and malignant tumors with appropriate margins. Biopsy Procedures: Taking tissue samples for diagnostic purposes. Abdominal Surgery: For conditions requiring internal exploration or organ removal. Exploratory Surgery: When a definitive diagnosis requires looking inside the body.

Our team, led by experienced and skilled veterinarians, is fully committed to providing the best possible surgical outcomes. We work closely with pet owners to explain procedures, discuss potential risks and benefits, and answer all your questions thoroughly. We believe that informed owners make the best decisions for their pets.

Before any surgical procedure, your pet will undergo a comprehensive physical examination and necessary pre-anesthetic blood work to ensure they are healthy enough for surgery. Advanced monitoring during anesthesia, including electrocardiogram (EKG), oxygen saturation, and temperature control, is standard practice at our facility.

We understand that entrusting your pet to us involves trust, and we are dedicated to earning and maintaining that trust. Our goal is not only to perform the necessary surgical intervention successfully but also to provide exceptional care throughout the entire process, ensuring your pet returns home safely and comfortably.

For appointments, consultations, or to learn more about our surgical services, please contact us directly. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What types of surgical procedures do you perform at your Phoenix location?

A: At our Dr. Kelly's Surgical Unit in Phoenix, we specialize in a wide range of surgical procedures. This includes routine spay and neuter surgeries, dental work (like tooth extractions), soft tissue surgery (removing foreign bodies, tumors, repairing wounds), orthopedic surgery (fracture repair, cruciate ligament stabilization), mass removals, biopsies, and exploratory surgery when needed for diagnosis.

Q2: How do I schedule a surgical consultation for my pet at your Phoenix facility?

A: You can schedule a surgical consultation by calling our phone number at (602) 641-4861. We will gather information about your pet's condition and schedule an appointment at our 4727 E Bell Rd #11 location in Phoenix. During the consultation, our veterinarian will discuss the recommended procedure, its benefits, risks, and recovery process.

Q3: What pre-surgical tests are required before my pet's operation at your clinic?

A: Generally, a thorough physical examination and pre-anesthetic blood work are required before any surgical procedure at our facility. These tests help us assess your pet's internal organ function and ensure they are healthy enough to safely undergo anesthesia and surgery. Specific requirements may vary depending on your pet's age and health status.

Q4: How long does it typically take for a pet to recover from surgery performed at your North Phoenix location?

A: Recovery time varies significantly depending on the type and complexity of the surgery, your pet's age, overall health, and individual healing capacity. Minor procedures like simple spays might have a recovery period of a few days at home, while more complex orthopedic surgeries can require several weeks of restricted activity and follow-up care. Our team will provide you with detailed post-operative instructions and follow-up appointments.

Q5: Do you offer pain management for pets undergoing surgery at your Phoenix practice?

A: Absolutely. Comprehensive pain management is a cornerstone of our surgical care philosophy at Dr. Kelly's Surgical Unit. We utilize a multimodal approach, which may include pre-operative sedation, local anesthetic blocks, intravenous pain medications during surgery, and a carefully planned post-operative pain management protocol. We are committed to keeping your pet comfortable and minimizing any discomfort throughout their recovery at our 4727 E Bell Rd #11 facility.
