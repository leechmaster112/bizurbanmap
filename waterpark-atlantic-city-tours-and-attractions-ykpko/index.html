Location

Waterpark Atlantic City Tours and Attractions is your premier destination for unforgettable aquatic fun right here in Atlantic City. Located at 2701 Pacific Ave, our waterpark offers a wide array of thrilling slides, family-friendly attractions, and splash zones designed to cater to visitors of all ages. We pride ourselves on providing a day of excitement and relaxation under one roof, making planning your visit simple and stress-free.

Planning your waterpark day doesn't have to be complicated. We offer various pass options to suit different needs, ensuring everyone can enjoy our facilities. From single-day admission to season passes, we have the perfect package for your visit. Our standard operating hours run from Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day weekend, typically opening early in the morning and staying open late into the evening to accommodate busy schedules. Please check our official website for the most current seasonal hours, as they may vary slightly throughout the summer months.

When deciding what to ride first, consider the thrill factor versus relaxation. Our park boasts several world-class slides, including high-speed tubers and winding family slides. The Wave Pool is a favorite for soaking up the sun and enjoying gentle splashes, while the Lazy River provides a leisurely float down scenic waterways. For the most excitement upfront, many visitors choose to tackle the park's tallest or fastest slide first thing in the morning to beat the crowds and maximize their adrenaline rush. However, families with younger children often prefer starting with calmer attractions like the kids' water play areas or the interactive waterfalls before moving to steeper drops.

Our commitment is to provide a clean, safe, and enjoyable environment for all guests visiting our waterpark at 2701 Pacific Ave. We employ trained staff to ensure guest safety and maintain high standards of cleanliness throughout the facility. Whether you're looking for a fun day out with friends, a family adventure, or a romantic escape with your partner featuring water massages and scenic views, our waterpark offers something for everyone right here in Atlantic City.

For detailed information on purchasing passes, viewing current hours, or getting directions to our location at 2701 Pacific Ave, please visit our official website: https://waterparkatlanticcity.org. You can also reach us directly by phone at (609) 555-2740 to ask specific questions or make arrangements.
