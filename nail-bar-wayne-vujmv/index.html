Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What services does Nail Bar Wayne offer?

A1: Nail Bar Wayne provides a comprehensive range of nail services, including classic manicures, pedicures, nail shaping, polish application, basic nail repairs, and various nail art designs. We focus on enhancing the natural beauty of your hands and feet.

Q2: How long does a typical manicure or pedicure take?

A2: A standard manicure usually takes around 30-45 minutes, while a pedicure can range from 45 minutes to an hour, depending on the chosen service and desired level of detail.

Q3: Can I schedule an appointment at Nail Bar Wayne?

A3: Yes, we recommend booking your appointment in advance to ensure availability, especially during busy periods. You can schedule by calling our phone number or checking our website.
