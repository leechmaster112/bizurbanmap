South Canyon Construction Inc

South Canyon Construction is a premier landscape contractor based in Orangevale inside of Sacramento County, California, specializing in transforming outdoor spaces into extraordinary, functional living areas. Serving homeowners throughout Placer, Sacramento, and Yolo counties, our team is committed to excellence and meticulous attention to detail. We pride ourselves on focusing on one project at a time, ensuring that each client receives our full dedication and the highest standard of quality and craftsmanship for their landscape. Our comprehensive services are designed to bring any outdoor vision to life, enhancing both beauty and property value. We offer expert landscape design, installation, and renovation, alongside a full suite of hardscape services including the installation of paver patios, pool decks, driveways, retaining walls, outdoor fireplaces, and custom stone veneer. Our expertise also extends to creating luxurious outdoor living environments with pool remodeling, water feature and sauna installations, artificial turf, and low-voltage lighting. As a licensed concrete contractor, we also provide practical solutions like French drain installation to protect your property. We are dedicated to creating beautiful, sustainable, and low-maintenance landscapes that our clients can enjoy for years to come. Our primary service areas include Sacramento, Orangevale, Carmichael, Folsom, El Dorado Hills, Roseville, Rocklin, Auburn, Lincoln, and the surrounding communities. At South Canyon Construction, we strive to exceed expectations on every job, delivering a finished product that blends aesthetics with functionality to boost your property's curb appeal and your enjoyment of it.
