Severin Cleaners

Severin Cleaners is a locally owned and operated junk removal and hauling company based in La Mesa, California, serving residential and commercial clients throughout San Diego County. Operating 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, Severin Cleaners specializes in full-service junk removal, providing fast, reliable, and professional solutions for removing unwanted items, debris, and clutter from homes, businesses, and properties of all sizes. With a strong focus on same-day service, transparent pricing, and eco-friendly disposal practices, Severin Cleaners has become a trusted local alternative to national junk removal franchises. The company offers comprehensive junk removal services designed to handle everything from single-item pickups to complete property cleanouts. Services include same-day and emergency junk removal, furniture removal such as couches, sectionals, mattresses, beds, tables, and chairs, and appliance removal including refrigerators, washers, dryers, dishwashers, stoves, and HVAC units. Severin Cleaners also performs full estate cleanouts for inherited properties, foreclosure and eviction cleanouts for banks and landlords, and hoarding cleanup services carried out with discretion, professionalism, and sensitivity. In addition to residential services, Severin Cleaners provides commercial junk removal for offices, retail spaces, warehouses, construction sites, and property management companies. Their construction debris removal services cover renovation and demolition waste, drywall, lumber, concrete, and scrap materials. Specialty removal services are also available, including hot tub removal, piano hauling, scrap metal removal, and heavy material disposal. Every job includes all labor, hauling, and post-removal cleanup, ensuring properties are left clean, clear, and ready for use. Severin Cleaners serves over 25 communities across San Diego County, including coastal areas such as La Jolla, Pacific Beach, Del Mar, Point Loma, Carlsbad, and Oceanside; central neighborhoods like North Park, Hillcrest, Clairemont, Kearny Mesa, and Mira Mesa; East County cities including La Mesa, El Cajon, Santee, Spring Valley, Lakeside, and Lemon Grove; South Bay areas such as Chula Vista; and North County locations including Vista, Escondido, Poway, San Marcos, Rancho Santa Fe, and Carmel Valley. Their deep local knowledge allows them to efficiently navigate neighborhood regulations, gated communities, HOAs, and luxury properties. Pricing at Severin Cleaners is volume-based and fully transparent, with upfront quotes provided before work begins. Rates include single-item pickups starting at $100, quarter-load, half-load, three-quarter-load, and full-truck options, with all costs covering labor, hauling, disposal fees, and cleanup. Final pricing is confirmed on-site prior to starting the job, and payment is collected only after the work is completed to the customer’s satisfaction. As a licensed and insured California junk removal business, Severin Cleaners prioritizes safety, professionalism, and environmental responsibility. The company coordinates donation of usable items whenever possible and emphasizes recycling and responsible disposal to minimize landfill waste. Crews are uniformed, trained, and respectful of client property, delivering consistent quality and attention to detail on every job. With a mission centered on “Restore, refresh, and reclaim,” Severin Cleaners focuses on delivering visible before-and-after transformations that turn cluttered, unusable spaces into clean, functional environments. Backed by over 200 satisfied customers, the company is known for its responsiveness, efficiency, and customer-first approach. Severin Cleaners can be reached at (619) 750-0114 or via email at severincleaners@gmail.com , and maintains an active online presence through its website, severincleaners.com, as well as Instagram, Facebook, Yelp, and Thumbtack, with SEO-optimized service pages for each community it serves.
