What is TMS Therapy?

Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) is a non-invasive procedure used to treat certain forms of depression. Unlike traditional antidepressant medications, TMS does not require ingesting pills. Instead, it uses focused magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain's prefrontal cortex, an area often underactive in individuals experiencing depression. This targeted stimulation can help restore normal brain function and alleviate depressive symptoms.

How Does TMS Work at MedPsych Integrated - Cary?

At our Cary location, our experienced team will first conduct a thorough assessment to determine if TMS is an appropriate treatment option for you. If recommended, the TMS sessions are typically held on an outpatient basis. You will remain awake and alert throughout the procedure, which is generally well-tolerated with minimal side effects compared to some other treatments. The TMS device generates a magnetic coil that produces a gentle tapping sound and a tapping sensation on the head. Each session lasts approximately 20-40 minutes and is usually scheduled five days a week for about four to six weeks.
