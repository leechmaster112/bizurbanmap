Frequently Asked Questions
Q1: What age is best to start orthodontic treatment?

A1: While treatment can be beneficial at any age, the American Association of Orthodontists recommends an initial orthodontic evaluation by age seven. This allows us to identify potential issues early and plan for the most effective treatment, which may involve early intervention or simply monitoring growth and development at our 280 Midpark Way SE Suite 208 location.

Q2: How long does orthodontic treatment typically take?

A2: The duration of orthodontic treatment varies depending on the complexity of the case and the individual's response to treatment. Most treatments at Impact Orthodontics SE take between 18 to 24 months, but some may be shorter or longer. We will provide a more personalized estimate after your initial consultation.

Q3: Will my child experience pain during orthodontic treatment?

A3: It's normal to experience some mild discomfort or soreness after braces are adjusted or new aligners are inserted. This is usually temporary and can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers. Our team at 280 Midpark Way SE Suite 208 prioritizes patient comfort throughout the treatment process.

Q4: What are the benefits of orthodontic treatment for my child?

A4: Beyond creating a beautiful smile, orthodontic treatment improves oral health by making teeth easier to clean, reducing the risk of cavities and gum disease. It also corrects bite problems, which can prevent jaw pain and improve chewing efficiency. Most importantly, a confident smile can significantly boost a child's self-esteem.
