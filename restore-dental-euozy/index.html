2. How can I schedule an appointment at Restore Dental?

You can schedule an appointment by calling our office at (310) 858-7373. Our team will assist you in finding a time that works best for you.

3. Does Restore Dental accept dental insurance?

Yes, we accept most major dental insurance plans. Please contact our office to discuss your specific coverage and benefits.

4. What is the process for getting dental implants?

Getting dental implants typically involves several steps, including a consultation, X-rays or scans, surgical placement of the implant, and a healing period followed by placement of the final restoration. Our team will guide you through each step.

We offer various payment options, including financing plans for larger procedures. Please inquire about our payment options when scheduling your appointment.
