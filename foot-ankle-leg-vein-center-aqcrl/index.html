Our Specialized Services

Our practice offers comprehensive care for various venous conditions. We begin with thorough evaluations to accurately diagnose your specific condition. Our expertise covers:

Varicose Veins

These are enlarged, twisted veins that typically appear in the legs. They can cause pain, swelling, and skin discoloration. Our treatments at 670 Glades Rd #320 include sclerotherapy, where a solution is injected into the vein to cause it to collapse and fade over time, and endovenous laser treatment (EVLT), which uses laser energy to close the affected vein. Both procedures are performed in our Boca Raton facility with minimal discomfort and quick recovery times.

Spider and Telangiectasia Veins

These are smaller, surface-level veins that appear as red or blue web-like patterns or thin lines on the skin. While often considered cosmetic, they can sometimes indicate underlying venous issues. We offer treatments like sclerotherapy at our Glades Road location to effectively diminish their appearance, leaving your skin looking smoother and more even.

Chronic Venous Insufficiency (CVI)

CVI occurs when the valves in the veins of the legs don't function properly, leading to blood pooling. Symptoms can include leg pain, swelling, skin changes, and ulcers. Our podiatrists at 670 Glades Rd #320 develop comprehensive management plans for CVI, which may include compression therapy, lifestyle modifications, and advanced procedures to improve blood flow and prevent complications.

Deep Vein Thrombosis (DVT)

DVT involves blood clots forming in the deep veins, usually in the legs. While our primary focus is on treating venous conditions, we are adept at recognizing symptoms of DVT and will refer you promptly to appropriate specialists if needed. Early diagnosis and treatment are crucial for DVT.

Lymphedema

This condition involves swelling, typically in the arms or legs, caused by a buildup of lymph fluid. While primarily a podiatry practice, we understand the connection between venous and lymphatic systems and can provide initial assessment and guidance for our Boca Raton patients. We may recommend specialized lymphedema treatment protocols or refer you to a lymphedema specialist.
