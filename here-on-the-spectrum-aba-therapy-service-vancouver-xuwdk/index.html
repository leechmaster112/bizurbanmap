What is ABA Therapy?

ABA, or Applied Behavior Analysis, is a scientific approach to understanding how human behavior is learned. It focuses on identifying the factors that influence a child's behavior and then uses positive reinforcement and other evidence-based techniques to teach new skills and reduce challenging behaviors. Our ABA therapy programs are tailored specifically to the needs of each child, focusing on areas such as communication, social skills, academic performance, daily living skills, and emotional regulation.
