Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of psychiatric services do you offer at your Colleyville location?

We provide comprehensive psychiatric evaluations and treatment for conditions including depression, anxiety disorders (such as generalized anxiety, panic disorder, social anxiety, and phobias), stress-related issues, sleep disorders, and substance use concerns. We also offer medication management for eligible patients.

Q2: Do you accept insurance for psychiatric services in Colleyville?

We work with many insurance providers to facilitate patient access to care. Please visit our website or call our Colleyville office at (817) 283-4300 to inquire about specific insurance plans we accept and the associated coverage details.

Q3: How long does a typical first appointment at your Colleyville practice take?

The initial appointment usually lasts approximately 90 minutes. This allows ample time for a thorough assessment, understanding your history and current concerns, and discussing potential treatment approaches.

Q4: What should I expect during a medication management session?

During a medication management visit, we will review your current symptoms, assess how medications are working (or not working), discuss any side effects, and make adjustments to your treatment plan as needed. These sessions typically last about 30-45 minutes.

Q5: Is therapy or counseling offered at your Colleyville clinic?

While our primary focus is medical psychiatry, we provide expert psychiatric diagnosis and medication management. We collaborate closely with therapists and other mental health professionals in the community to ensure comprehensive care, though we do not offer individual psychotherapy sessions ourselves at this location.
