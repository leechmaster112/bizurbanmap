Location

At Modern Mind Clinic, we provide a modern approach to mental wellness, offering specialized treatments for those seeking advanced care. Our clinic at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750, Southfield, MI 48075, is designed to be a sanctuary for healing and progress. We understand that traditional treatments are not always effective for everyone, which is why we focus on innovative solutions like TMS Therapy and Spravato treatment, complemented by comprehensive telehealth mental health services. Our mission is to deliver cutting-edge care in a comfortable and professional setting, helping our patients achieve a brighter state of mind.

Our Southfield clinic is the heart of our operations, where our dedicated team of mental health professionals works with you to create a personalized treatment plan. TMS Therapy, or Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation, is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain to improve symptoms of depression. This FDA-approved treatment is offered on-site at our Southfield location, providing a valuable option for patients who have not found success with medication alone. Similarly, Spravato (esketamine) nasal spray is administered in our comfortable and supervised clinical setting at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750, offering a new path forward for treatment-resistant depression.

Understanding the need for accessible care, Modern Mind Clinic also provides robust telehealth mental health services. This allows individuals to connect with our providers from the comfort and privacy of their own homes, ensuring continuity of care and making mental health support more convenient than ever. Whether you visit us in person at our Southfield office or connect with us virtually, you receive the same high standard of modern, compassionate care. Our goal is to make effective mental health treatment accessible to the community we serve directly from 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750.

Choosing Modern Mind Clinic means choosing a partner dedicated to your mental well-being. FAQ

What TMS Therapy services are offered at your Southfield clinic?

Our Southfield clinic at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750 provides FDA-cleared Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS Therapy) for major depressive disorder. This non-invasive treatment is conducted in-office under the supervision of our clinical team.

How do I receive Spravato treatment at your location?

Spravato treatment is administered on-site at our Southfield office located at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750. After a consultation, patients receive the treatment in a comfortable, private room and are monitored by our staff throughout the session.

Are telehealth appointments available for residents?

Yes, Modern Mind Clinic offers telehealth mental health services for patients. These virtual appointments allow you to meet with our providers from your home, providing flexible and accessible care from our Southfield-based clinic.

What is the first step to starting treatment at your clinic?

The first step is to schedule a consultation to discuss your needs and determine the best treatment plan. You can initiate this process by calling our Southfield office at (248) 607-7190 or visiting our website to request an appointment.

Do I need a referral to become a patient at Modern Mind Clinic?

While a referral from your current physician or psychiatrist can be helpful, it is not required. You can contact our Southfield clinic directly at (248) 607-7190 to schedule an initial evaluation and learn more about our services.
