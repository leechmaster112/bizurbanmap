Our Core Services

Lawn Mowing & Edging

Consistent, precise lawn mowing is essential for maintaining a neat appearance and promoting healthy grass growth. Our lawn mowing service includes not only cutting the grass to the desired height but also meticulous edging around flower beds, driveways, and sidewalks. We utilize professional-grade equipment to ensure a clean cut every time, leaving your lawn looking sharp and well-tended.

Trimming & Shaping

Overgrown hedges, unruly shrubs, and untamed flower beds detract from the overall beauty of your landscape. Our trimming services focus on precision and artistry, shaping vegetation to complement your home's architecture and personal style. Whether it's formal hedges, natural shrubs, or manicured ground covers, our team ensures clean cuts and a polished finish.

Lawn Fertilization & Soil Health

A healthy lawn starts with healthy soil. Our fertilization program is customized to address the specific nutrient needs of your lawn, taking into account factors like soil type and seasonal growth patterns common in Alabaster. Regular fertilization promotes deeper root growth, enhances color, and improves drought tolerance, ensuring your lawn thrives year-round.

Basic Landscaping & Mulching

Enhance the natural beauty of your property with our basic landscaping services. This includes mulching around trees and garden beds, which helps retain soil moisture, suppress weeds, and improve soil structure. We also offer guidance on selecting appropriate plants and ground covers suited to the Alabaster climate, contributing to a sustainable and attractive landscape.

Seasonal Cleanup & Preparation

Prep your landscape for seasonal changes with our cleanup and preparation services. In the spring, we remove winter debris and prepare your lawn for the growing season. In the fall, we help clear leaves and prepare your landscape for winter. These services ensure your property starts each season in optimal condition.
