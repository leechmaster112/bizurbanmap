Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What types of garage doors do you install and repair?

A1: We service and repair a wide range of garage door types, including standard sectional doors (up to 14ft wide), rolling steel doors, wood carriage doors, and commercial-grade doors. We work with major brands and can source parts or recommend suitable replacements.

Q2: How long does a typical garage door repair take?

A2: The duration depends on the complexity of the issue. Many common repairs, like replacing a broken spring or troubleshooting an opener, can often be completed in under an hour. More extensive repairs might take a bit longer, but our team works efficiently to minimize your downtime.

Q3: Do you offer emergency garage door repair services?

A3: Yes, we understand that a broken garage door, especially one that's locked or inoperable, can be a significant inconvenience. We strive to provide prompt service for emergency repairs. While same-day appointments are often possible, please call us at (615) 378-7022 as soon as possible.

Q4: How often should I get my garage door serviced?

A4: We recommend professional garage door inspections and maintenance at least once a year. Regular servicing helps identify potential issues early, ensures smooth operation, extends the lifespan of your door and opener, and maintains proper safety features.

Q5: What should I do if my garage door opener isn't working?

A5: If your garage door opener isn't working, start by checking the circuit breaker or fuse box for your opener. Also, ensure the door is fully closed and check if the wall control buttons are working. If these steps don't resolve the issue, or if the opener itself is malfunctioning, please contact our technicians at (615) 378-7022 for a diagnosis and repair.
