Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What exactly is a slab foundation, and why might it need repair in Houston?

A slab foundation is a concrete base built directly on the ground. In Houston, factors like expansive clay soil, heavy rainfall, poor drainage, and aging can cause cracks, shifting, or settling, necessitating repair to prevent further damage.

Q2: How do I know if my Houston home's slab foundation needs professional repair?

Signs include visible cracks in the slab (especially wider than 1/8 inch), sticking doors/windows, cracks in walls or floors, uneven floors, gaps between the slab and house walls, or structural movement. Contacting a local expert like Texas Slab Guys is recommended for an accurate assessment.

Q3: What is the typical process for slab foundation repair in Houston?

The process usually involves an inspection, a detailed estimate, preparation of the area, specialized repair techniques (like mudjacking, slab jacking, or full slab replacement depending on the issue), and final cleanup. Texas Slab Guys will guide you through each step.

Q4: How long does a typical slab foundation repair project take in Houston?

The duration varies based on the extent of the damage and the chosen repair method. Minor repairs might be completed in a day, while more complex issues could take several days to a week. Texas Slab Guys will provide a timeline during your consultation.
