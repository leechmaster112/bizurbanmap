Location

The Harewood Dental Clinic, located at 521 Deering St, Nanaimo, BC V9R 6Y2, stands as a cornerstone of comprehensive dental care for the residents of this vibrant community. For over two decades, our dedicated team has been committed to providing exceptional, personalized dental services tailored to the unique needs of patients of all ages right here in Nanaimo. We understand that a healthy smile is fundamental to overall well-being and confidence, and we strive to make quality dental care accessible, comfortable, and effective for everyone who walks through our doors.

At our Nanaimo clinic, we offer a full spectrum of dental services designed to address a wide range of oral health concerns. Our experienced dental professionals utilize the latest techniques and technology to ensure you receive the highest standard of care in a welcoming and modern environment.

Comprehensive General Dentistry

Our foundation of care includes essential services like regular dental check-ups and professional teeth cleanings. These routine visits are crucial for maintaining optimal oral health, preventing dental diseases, and catching any potential issues early. We also provide fillings for cavities caused by tooth decay, ensuring your teeth are restored effectively. Root Canal Therapy is another key service we offer, utilizing advanced techniques to save infected teeth and relieve often intense pain, preserving your natural smile.

Cosmetic and Restorative Dentistry

For patients seeking to enhance the appearance and function of their smiles, Harewood Dental Clinic offers a range of cosmetic and restorative options. We provide Dental Crowns to protect weakened teeth, restore damaged teeth, or following root canal treatment. Dental Bridges can replace one or more missing teeth, providing a natural-looking solution. For those looking to replace multiple missing teeth, we offer Fixed Dental Implants, a durable and natural-feeling option. We also address common issues like worn or chipped teeth with Cosmetic Bonding, using a tooth-coloured resin to repair minor defects and improve aesthetics.

Specialized Care for Optimal Health

We understand that oral health extends beyond just the mouth. Our clinic provides services like Gum Therapy (Periodontal Care) to treat gum disease and promote gum health, which is linked to overall systemic wellness. We also offer Inlays and Onlays for stronger, more conservative restorations of larger cavities than a simple filling allows.

Technology and Comfort

We believe in providing the most comfortable and efficient patient experience. Our state-of-the-art facility is equipped with modern dental technology, including digital X-rays for clearer images and reduced radiation exposure, and possibly Intraoral Cameras to help you visualize your teeth and understand the treatment required. Our focus is on patient comfort, and we utilize techniques like rubber dams during procedures to ensure a clean and bacteria-free environment.

Choosing Harewood Dental Clinic means choosing a practice deeply rooted in the Nanaimo community. We are committed to building lasting relationships with our patients, offering gentle care, clear communication, and personalized treatment plans. Your oral health is our priority, and we are dedicated to helping you achieve and maintain a healthy, beautiful smile for life.

For more information about our services or to schedule your appointment, please visit our website at https://www.harewooddentalclinic.ca or call us directly at (250) 754-1949.
