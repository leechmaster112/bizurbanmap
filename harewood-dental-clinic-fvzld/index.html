Location

Harewood Dental Clinic, located at 521 Deering St, Nanaimo, BC V9R 6Y2, is dedicated to providing comprehensive and compassionate dental care right here in our community. We understand that maintaining good oral health is essential for overall well-being, and we are committed to helping our patients achieve and maintain healthy smiles through personalized care and state-of-the-art dental technology.

At Harewood Dental Clinic, our experienced team of dental professionals is dedicated to delivering high-quality dental services tailored to the unique needs of patients of all ages. We pride ourselves on creating a comfortable and welcoming environment where patients feel at ease during their dental visits. Whether you require a routine check-up or a more complex dental procedure, you can trust us to provide expert care with the latest advancements in dental techniques and materials.

Our comprehensive range of dental services includes preventative care, restorative treatments, and cosmetic dentistry, all provided under one roof at our Nanaimo location. We believe in a holistic approach to dental health, focusing not only on treating existing conditions but also on educating our patients about maintaining optimal oral hygiene at home.

Preventative Care:

Dental Examinations: Regular check-ups are crucial for maintaining oral health. Our thorough examinations help us detect potential issues early, when they are most easily and cost-effectively treated. Dental Cleanings: Professional cleanings remove plaque and tartar buildup that cannot be eliminated by brushing and flossing alone, helping to prevent gum disease. Dental X-rays: When necessary, we use digital X-rays to get a clear view of your teeth and supporting structures, aiding in accurate diagnosis. Oral Hygiene Guidance: We provide personalized advice on brushing, flossing, and other oral hygiene practices to help you maintain a healthy smile between dental visits.

Restorative Dentistry:

Fillings: We treat cavities with durable and aesthetic fillings to restore the function and appearance of your teeth. Dental Crowns: Crowns are used to protect and restore damaged or weakened teeth, providing a natural-looking and functional solution. Root Canal Therapy: When the pulp inside a tooth becomes infected, root canal treatment can save the tooth and relieve pain. Extractions: In cases where a tooth cannot be saved, our gentle extraction techniques ensure minimal discomfort. Teeth Grinding (Bruxism) Treatment: We offer solutions for patients who grind or clench their teeth, including custom mouthguards.

Cosmetic Dentistry:

Dental Veneers: Veneers can improve the appearance of your smile by covering imperfections such as chips, cracks, or gaps. Teeth Whitening: Our professional whitening treatments can brighten your smile and enhance your natural tooth colour. Dental Bonding: This procedure uses a tooth-coloured resin to repair minor chips, cracks, or gaps in your teeth.

We understand that finding the right dental care provider is important, and we are committed to making your experience positive from your first contact with our team. Our friendly and knowledgeable staff are always ready to answer your questions and address any concerns you may have about your dental health.

At Harewood Dental Clinic, we believe that excellent communication and patient education are key to successful dental outcomes. We take the time to explain your treatment options, answer your questions, and ensure you feel comfortable and informed about your care.

Whether you are a new patient needing to establish care or a returning patient requiring ongoing treatment, we welcome you to our practice at 521 Deering St, Nanaimo, BC V9R 6Y2. We are dedicated to providing the highest standard of dental care in a friendly and supportive environment.
