Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs):

1. What services does Bubbly Laundromat offer?

We offer washing and drying services for all types of fabrics and loads. We provide both small and large capacity machines to accommodate various needs.

Bubbly Laundromat is open from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM, seven days a week, including holidays. Please check our website or call us for any updates.

Yes, we accept credit cards for all transactions. We also accept cash and digital payment methods.

Occasionally, we run promotions and discounts for regular customers. You can check our website or call us to inquire about current offers.
