Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What exactly does a Gold IRA Custodian do?

A Gold IRA Custodian, like Gold IRA Custodians, is a financial institution licensed and qualified to hold assets within a Self-Directed IRA. Our role includes opening the account, ensuring compliance with IRS regulations, facilitating the transfer or rollover of funds, managing the purchase and secure storage of your precious metals investments, and handling all administrative paperwork. We act as the intermediary, making it legal and safe for you to invest in physical gold, silver, platinum, and palladium within your retirement account.

Why can't I just buy gold myself and put it in my IRA?

The IRS has specific rules regarding what assets can be held within a traditional or Roth IRA. While you can own gold, it must be held by a qualified custodian like Gold IRA Custodians. Attempting to hold physical gold personally within a standard IRA structure can lead to penalties and the loss of tax-deferred or tax-free growth benefits. A custodian ensures your investments remain compliant and your retirement funds are protected.

What types of precious metals can I invest in with a Gold IRA?

Gold IRA Custodians facilitates investments in physical precious metals approved by the IRS for retirement accounts.
