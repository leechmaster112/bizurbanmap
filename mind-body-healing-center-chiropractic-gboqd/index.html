What is Chiropractic Care?

Chiropractic is a healthcare profession focused on the relationship between the spine and nervous system and their impact on the overall health of the patient. Our approach at Mind Body Healing Center Chiropractic in Everett is centered on the principle that proper alignment of the spine is essential for optimal nervous system function, which in turn affects every cell, tissue, and organ in your body.

Our Specialized Approach to Auto Accident Injuries

Auto accidents often cause misalignments of the spine, particularly in the neck and upper back, due to the forceful impact. These misalignations, known as subluxations, can interfere with nerve signals traveling to and from the brain, potentially leading to pain, restricted mobility, and other symptoms long after the accident. Our Everett chiropractic practice specializes in identifying and correcting these misalignations safely and effectively.
