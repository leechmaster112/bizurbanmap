Frequently Asked Questions

1. What types of chiropractic services do you offer for car accident injuries?

We specialize in post-auto accident chiropractic care, including spinal adjustments to address misalignments caused by trauma, soft tissue therapy to relieve muscle tension and pain, and guidance on rehabilitation exercises to promote healing and restore function.

2. How soon after an accident should I seek chiropractic care?

It is beneficial to seek chiropractic care as soon as possible after an auto accident. Delaying treatment may allow soft tissue injuries to become more severe and chronic. Early intervention helps manage pain and prevent long-term complications.

3. Do you accept insurance?

We work with various insurance providers. Please contact our Everett office at +1 425-339-6000 to discuss your specific insurance coverage and verify if our services are accepted.

4. What should I expect during my first chiropractic appointment?

Your first appointment will involve a thorough consultation to understand your medical history and the details of your accident. We will perform a physical examination, which may include spinal palpation and range of motion tests. Based on our findings, we will create a personalized treatment plan and may begin your first adjustment if appropriate.

5. Is chiropractic care covered by auto accident insurance?

Depending on the specifics of your auto accident and the insurance policy involved (including PIP, Personal Injury Protection), chiropractic care may be covered. We recommend discussing your coverage with our office staff, who can assist you in understanding your benefits.
