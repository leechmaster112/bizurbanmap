Do you work with foreign buyers purchasing property in Mexico?

Absolutely. We specialize in assisting foreign buyers with the complexities of purchasing property in Mexico, including understanding the fideicomiso (bank trust) process, taxes, and legal requirements. Our bilingual team ensures a smooth transaction.

What is the process for buying property in Cabo San Lucas?

The process typically involves an initial consultation, property search and selection, due diligence (inspection and title check), financing arrangements (if needed), signing contracts, and closing with a fideicomiso. We guide you through each step.
