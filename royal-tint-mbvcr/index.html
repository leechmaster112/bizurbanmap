2. How long does a typical window tint installation take at your 5600 Pacific St facility?

Most standard vehicle tint jobs are completed within 2 to 4 hours at our Rocklin bay. The exact time can vary depending on the vehicle type and the number of windows being tinted. We prioritize a perfect, bubble-free installation over speed to ensure a royal finish.

3. Does Royal Tint provide paint protection film services in Rocklin?

Yes, as premier protection specialists, we expertly install paint protection film (PPF) at our Rocklin location. This clear, durable film is applied to the vulnerable surfaces of your vehicle to protect the paint from chips, scratches, and environmental contaminants.

4. Are the window tints you use at your Rocklin location legal for California?

We ensure all our tint installations comply with California state laws. During your consultation at our Pacific St shop, we will clearly explain the legal tint darkness (VLT%) for your specific vehicle to ensure full compliance.

5. How do I care for my new tint or paint protection film after leaving your Rocklin shop?

We provide specific aftercare instructions for all our services. Generally, for window tint, we advise avoiding rolling down windows and cleaning the glass for a few days. For paint protection film, we recommend using gentle, ammonia-free cleaners. Our team at Royal Tint will give you detailed care guidelines upon completion of your service.
