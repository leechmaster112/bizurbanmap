Christophe Viseux Wedding Photography

Christophe Viseux is a documentary wedding photographer in Ottawa. Unposed, unscripted, and quiet enough that you forget he is in the room, he photographs the real moments of your day rather than staging them. Fourteen years, more than 300 weddings and over 150 international awards, with work published in Vogue, Harper's Bazaar and WedLuxe. Coverage across Ottawa, Gatineau and Eastern Ontario, at venues including Château Laurier, Stonefields, Le Belvédère, Strathmere, Evermore and the National Arts Centre. One wedding per weekend, so the photographer you meet is the one at the back of your ceremony. Multicultural, interfaith and same-sex weddings welcome, in French or English. Galleries delivered in two to three weeks.
