Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What ages are allowed to rent a jet ski?

A: Typically, riders must be at least 18 years old and possess a valid driver's license. Minors usually require parental consent and accompaniment. Specific age and licensing requirements can be confirmed when booking.

Q: Do I need to make a reservation?

A: While walk-ins are often welcome, especially during off-peak hours, it's highly recommended to call us at (305) 384-6648 or book online via our website https://jetskirentalmiamifl.com/ for guaranteed availability, especially during busy tourist seasons or weekends.

Q: What safety equipment is provided?

A: We provide all necessary safety equipment, including a properly fitted life jacket (Personal Flotation Device or PFD) for each rider. Helmets are not typically required for jet ski operation in Florida waters but are available upon request.

Q: What is the operating season?

A: Jet ski rentals are available year-round at our Miami Beach location, subject to weather conditions and water temperature. However, the peak season with optimal conditions is generally from November through April.

Q: Can I ride the jet ski anywhere in the water?

A: While we encourage exploring the beautiful waters around Miami Beach, please respect any posted no-wake zones, marina regulations, and environmental guidelines. Adhere to local boating rules and regulations at all times for safety and courtesy.
