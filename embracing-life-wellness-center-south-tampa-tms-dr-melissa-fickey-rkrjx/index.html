Frequently Asked Questions for Our Tampa Location

1. What is TMS therapy and how is it performed at your South Tampa office? TMS, or Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation, is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain. At our clinic at 3333 W Kennedy Blvd #106, you will sit in a comfortable chair while a magnetic coil is gently placed against your scalp. The treatment is focused on the prefrontal cortex and is completely outpatient, allowing you to drive yourself to and from your appointments.

2. How do I know if TMS at your Tampa location is right for me? TMS therapy is typically recommended for adults who have struggled with depression and have not experienced satisfactory improvement from prior antidepressant medication. The best way to determine if you are a candidate is to schedule a consultation with Dr. Melissa Fickey at our South Tampa TMS center. She will conduct a thorough assessment to see if TMS is a suitable option for your specific situation.

3. What can I expect during a typical treatment session at 3333 W Kennedy Blvd #106? Each TMS session at our Embracing Life Wellness Center is designed to be comfortable and convenient. After an initial mapping session to determine the correct placement and dosage, subsequent sessions are brief. You will be awake and alert throughout the approximately 20-minute procedure. Many patients choose to relax, listen to music, or simply rest during their treatment before resuming their normal daily schedule.

4. Are the services at Embracing Life Wellness Center covered by insurance? TMS therapy is covered by most major insurance providers for the treatment of Major Depressive Disorder. Our team at the South Tampa TMS office will work directly with your insurance company to verify your benefits and outline any out-of-pocket costs before you begin your treatment. We are committed to making this life-changing therapy as accessible as possible.
