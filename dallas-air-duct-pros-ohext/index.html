Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: How often should I get my air ducts cleaned?

A1: While the National Air Duct Cleaners Association (NADCA) recommends duct cleaning every 3-5 years for most homes, factors like allergies, smoking in the home, recent renovations, or pest infestations might warrant more frequent cleanings. Our team at Dallas Air Duct Pros can assess your specific situation and recommend the best cleaning schedule.

Q2: What does a professional duct cleaning involve?

A2: A professional cleaning typically includes inspecting the accessible ductwork, using specialized tools to dislodge debris, and employing powerful vacuums to remove the loosened particles. We clean supply and return ducts, registers, grilles, and coils, focusing on removing accumulated dust, pollen, mold, mildew, and other contaminants from our Dallas location.
