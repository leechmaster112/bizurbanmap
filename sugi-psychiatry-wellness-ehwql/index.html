Sugi Psychiatry & Wellness

Sugi Psychiatry & Wellness is a mental health clinic serving Fernley, NV, offering comprehensive psychiatric care for adolescents and adults. Services include psychiatric evaluations, medication management, therapy, and evidence-based treatments for conditions such as anxiety, depression, ADHD, bipolar disorder, PTSD, and OCD. The practice is staffed by board-certified psychiatrists and experienced mental health providers and focuses on personalized, patient-centered care to improve overall quality of life.
