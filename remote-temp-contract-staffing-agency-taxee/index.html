Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of remote roles do you place?

We place a wide range of remote roles, including administrative support, customer service, technical roles (IT, software development), marketing, finance, project management, and more. Our focus is on connecting businesses with qualified remote professionals for various skill levels and industries.

Q2: How quickly can you place staff for an urgent need?

We understand the importance of speed, especially for urgent staffing needs. Our extensive database and proactive sourcing approach allows us to often identify and present suitable candidates within 24-48 hours, depending on the specific requirements and availability.

Q3: Do you handle the entire onboarding process for temporary staff?

Yes, we work closely with both the client and the temporary employee to ensure a smooth onboarding process. This includes providing necessary paperwork, setting clear expectations, and facilitating communication to ensure a productive working relationship.

Q4: Can I convert a temporary assignment to a permanent position through your agency?

Absolutely. We specialize in temp-to-hire placements. If you find a temporary employee who performs exceptionally well and you decide to offer them a permanent position, we can assist with the transition and support the hiring process.

Q5: Are your remote staff vetted and reliable?

Yes, reliability and quality are paramount. We conduct thorough vetting, including skills assessments, background checks, and reference verification for all candidates we present. We only recommend professionals who meet our high standards and have a proven track record.
