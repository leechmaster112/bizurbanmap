Our Services: Assessment and Therapy

At Articulate Kids, we begin with a thorough assessment to understand the child's unique communication profile. This may involve evaluating their speech sounds, language comprehension and expression, social communication skills, and how they approach literacy. Based on this assessment, we develop an individualised therapy plan focused on the specific goals identified by the child, parents, and, where appropriate, the child's school. Our therapy approaches are evidence-based and tailored to be engaging and effective for children in Petts Wood. We work collaboratively with parents and caregivers, providing practical strategies and support to help children generalise skills learned in therapy to everyday situations at home and school.

Conditions We Support

We address a range of speech and language difficulties commonly seen in children living locally:

Articulation and Phonology: Difficulty producing specific speech sounds clearly. Language Impairment: Challenges with understanding spoken language or expressing thoughts and ideas verbally or in writing. Stuttering: Disfluencies in speech, such as repetitions or blocks. Receptive and Expressive Language Delays: Significant lag in developing listening and talking skills compared to peers. Social Communication Difficulties: Struggles with understanding and using the skills needed for effective interaction and relationships. Apraxia of Speech: A motor planning disorder affecting the ability to produce speech sounds accurately and in sequence. Reading and Spelling Difficulties (Dyslexia): Challenges related to language processing that impact literacy skills.

Our therapists are dedicated to creating a supportive and encouraging environment where children feel understood and empowered to develop their communication skills.
