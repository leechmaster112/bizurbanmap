However, selection is often tied to what is available at dealerships.

  • Description: The official certified pre-owned program offered directly by Mercedes-Benz, providing factory-backed warranties and rigorous inspections.
  • Pros:

    • Rigorous inspection process.

  • Cons:

  • Who it's best for: Buyers who highly value the security of a factory-backed warranty and prefer purchasing directly from a Mercedes-Benz dealership.
