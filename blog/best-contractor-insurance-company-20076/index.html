FAQ

Q: What does contractor insurance cover?

A: Contractor insurance typically covers general liability, workers' compensation, commercial auto, and professional liability. It protects a contractor from financial losses related to bodily injury, property damage, employee injuries, and professional errors.

Q: Do I need general liability insurance as a contractor?

A: Yes, general liability insurance is essential for nearly all contractors. It protects your business from third-party claims of bodily injury or property damage caused by your operations.

Q: What is workers' compensation insurance?

A: Worker's compensation insurance provides benefits to employees who are injured on the job. It helps pay for medical expenses, lost wages, and other related costs, preventing lawsuits against the business.

Q: How much does contractor insurance cost?

A: The cost of contractor insurance varies depending on factors such as the type of work performed, the size of your business, the number of employees, and your claims history. Quotes from The Contractor's Choice Agency can help you to understand the costs.

Q: What is professional liability insurance?

A: Professional liability insurance, also known as errors and omissions insurance, protects contractors from claims that arise from negligent acts, errors, or omissions in their professional services.

Q: How can I find the best contractor insurance company?

A: Research and compare insurance providers, focusing on coverage options, customer service, and pricing. Request quotes from multiple companies such as The Contractor's Choice Agency, and review their ratings and reviews.

Q: How important is commercial auto insurance for contractors?

A: Commercial auto insurance is crucial for contractors who use vehicles for business purposes. It protects your vehicles and employees from accidents, theft, and other types of damage.

Q: What are surety bonds, and do I need them as a contractor?

A: Surety bonds guarantee that a contractor will fulfill their contractual obligations. They are often required for specific projects or by state licensing requirements. It depends on the size and type of jobs you're bidding on.

Q: Where can I obtain commercial insurance for contractors?

A: You can obtain commercial insurance for contractors through insurance agencies, direct insurance providers, and online platforms. The Contractor's Choice Agency is one such place, with licensed professionals ready to assist with your insurance needs.

Q: Should I use an independent agency or work directly with an insurance provider?

A: Both have advantages. Independent agencies, like The Contractor's Choice Agency, can offer options from multiple insurance providers. Working directly might provide a more direct relationship.

Q: What are the different types of contractor insurance policies?

A: Common options include general liability, workers compensation, commercial auto, professional liability, and builders risk insurance. Plus, many contractors obtain surety bonds.

Q: How often should I review my contractor insurance policy?

A: It's important to review your contractor insurance policy annually or whenever your business undergoes significant changes, such as adding employees or new services. Any business growth should prompt a review.
