FAQ

Q1: What is the difference between PPF and a car wrap?
A: PPF (Paint Protection Film) is a clear, protective film designed to shield your car's paint from rock chips and other road hazards, while a car wrap is a vinyl film applied to change the color or add a design to your vehicle. Both protect but in different ways.

Q2: How long does PPF last?
A: High-quality PPF, like the kind used by Dipped Auto Works, can last for many years, often 5-10 years, with proper care.

Q3: Can a car wrap damage my car's paint?
A: No, car wraps by a reputable shop like Dipped Auto Works, are designed to protect the underlying paint. In fact, they can provide an extra layer of protection from the elements.

Q4: How much does PPF cost?
A: The cost varies depending on the coverage area (partial vs. full vehicle) and the quality of the film. It's best to get a quote specific to your vehicle from a shop like Dipped Auto Works.

Q5: What are the benefits of a full car wrap?
A: Full car wraps offer complete paint protection, a new look for your car, and easy removal if you want to revert to the original paint color. Great for showcasing a brand like yours!

Q6: Can I wash a car with PPF?
A: Yes, you can wash a car with PPF as you normally would. However, avoid abrasive cleaners and pressure washers used too closely.

Q7: How do I choose the right car wrap shop in Las Vegas?
A: Look for shops with a proven track record, positive customer reviews, experience with your vehicle's make and model, and use of high-quality materials. Dipped Auto Works is a great option.

Q8: How does PPF work to protect my car?
A: PPF is a self-healing film that absorbs impacts from rock chips, scratches, and other abrasions, protecting your vehicle's original paint. This is something offered at Dipped Auto Works.

Q10: What types of vinyl wraps are available?
A: There's a wide range, including matte, gloss, satin, carbon fiber, chrome, and custom printed designs; Dipped Auto Works offers a comprehensive selection.

Q11: Will the wrapping affect the resale value of my car?
A: A well-installed wrap can preserve the original paint, boosting resale value. Removal is easy, too.

Q12: How long does it take to install PPF?
