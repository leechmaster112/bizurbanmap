Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What should I do immediately after a car accident in Calgary?
A: Ensure your safety and the safety of others. Call the police, exchange information with the other driver(s), gather witness contact information, and seek medical attention, even if you don't feel injured immediately after a collision. Document everything.

Q: How much does it cost to hire one of the best car accident lawyers in Calgary?
A: Most personal injury lawyers, including those at Preszler Injury Lawyers, work on a contingency fee basis. This means you only pay legal fees if they successfully secure compensation for your case. The fees are typically a percentage of the settlement or court award.

Q: How long do I have to file a claim after a car accident in Alberta?
A: In Alberta, you generally have two years from the date of the accident to file a claim for personal injury. It's crucial to consult with a lawyer as soon as possible, as gathering evidence and building a strong case takes time.

Q: What types of damages can I claim after a car accident?
A: You can claim various types of damages, including medical expenses, lost wages, pain and suffering, and the cost of repairing or replacing your damaged vehicle. The specific damages depend on the details of your case.

Q: How does a lawyer investigate a car accident claim?
A: A lawyer will investigate by gathering police reports, interviewing witnesses, collecting medical records, and examining the accident scene. They will also gather evidence to demonstrate the other party's negligence.

Q: What role does insurance play in a car accident claim?
A: Insurance companies often take a very active role. Your lawyer will deal with the insurance company on your behalf. They negotiate settlements and, if necessary, take the case to court to obtain fair compensation.

Q: Why should I hire a car accident lawyer instead of dealing with the insurance company myself?
A: Insurance companies aim to minimize payouts. A lawyer advocates for your best interests. Lawyers have experience navigating the legal process, assessing the full extent of your damages, and negotiating a fair settlement.

Q: What are the benefits of choosing a local Calgary car accident lawyer?
A: Local lawyers have a better understanding of Alberta's laws and court procedures. They also are familiar with local insurance companies and have established relationships within the community. They can also meet with you personally.

Q: Are all car accident lawyers in Calgary equal?
A: No. Experience, specialization, and resources vary greatly. Research and choose a lawyer with a strong track record, a focus on personal injury, and a commitment to client service.
