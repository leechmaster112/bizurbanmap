FAQ

Here are some frequently asked questions about hiring a private investigator in Melbourne:

  1. What types of cases do private investigators in Melbourne handle?

    Private investigators in Melbourne handle a diverse range of cases, including infidelity investigations, missing persons, background checks, surveillance, corporate investigations (fraud, theft, employee misconduct), asset tracing, and family law matters.

  2. How much does it cost to hire a private investigator in Melbourne?

    The cost of hiring a private investigator in Melbourne varies based on the complexity of the case, the scope of work, and the firm’s rates. Costs can be hourly, or based on the type of investigation, surveillance and the time it takes.

  3. Is it legal to hire a private investigator in Melbourne?

    Yes, it is legal to hire a private investigator in Melbourne, provided they operate within the bounds of the law. They must be licensed and adhere to privacy laws and regulations.

  4. How long does an investigation take?

    The duration of an investigation depends on several factors, including the type of case, the level of complexity, and the amount of information available. Some cases may be resolved quickly, while others can take weeks or months.

  5. How do I choose a private investigator in Melbourne?

    When choosing a private investigator, consider experience, reputation, services offered, licensing, specialization, and client reviews. Always check references and ensure they are licensed in Victoria.

  6. What information do I need to provide to a private investigator?

    You’ll need to provide as much relevant information as possible, including the names of the subjects, any known addresses, dates of birth, vehicle information, and any background information that may be relevant to the case. The more information, the better.

  7. What are the benefits of hiring a private investigator?

    Private investigators can gather evidence, conduct surveillance, locate missing persons, provide background checks, and offer insight and clarity on complex situations, they can also provide you with access to court admissible evidence.

  8. Can a private investigator provide evidence for court?

    Yes, private investigators can gather evidence that may be admissible in court. It depends on the case, and they must adhere to all legal requirements for gathering and presenting the evidence.

  9. Are private investigators in Melbourne licensed?

    Yes, private investigators in Melbourne must hold a private security licence issued by the state government, in order to operate legally.
