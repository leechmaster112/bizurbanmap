What does a private investigator in Sydney do?

How much does a private investigator cost in Sydney?

The cost of a private investigator in Sydney varies depending on the type and complexity of the investigation. Fees can be hourly, per project, or a combination of both. Some firms offer packages.

How do I find a reliable private investigator in Sydney?

Look for firms with a strong reputation, experienced investigators, and clear communication. Check online reviews, request references, and ensure they are licensed and insured.

What types of cases do private investigators handle in Sydney?

Private investigators in Sydney handle various cases, including infidelity investigations, missing persons cases, child custody disputes, fraud investigations, and corporate investigations.

How long does a private investigation take in Sydney?

The duration of a private investigation in Sydney depends on the case's complexity and scope. Some investigations may take a few days or weeks, while others could require several months. Discuss timelines with the investigator.

Are private investigators in Sydney licensed?

Yes, private investigators in Sydney are required to be licensed by the relevant authorities. This ensures they adhere to legal and ethical standards.

What information do I need to provide to a private investigator?

To provide all available relevant information is very important. You should typically provide as much information as possible, including names, dates, addresses, and any supporting documentation, to assist the investigation process. Confidentiality is key.

Can a private investigator in Sydney use surveillance?

Yes, private investigators in Sydney can use surveillance techniques, but they must adhere to strict legal and ethical guidelines regarding privacy and data collection. Surveillance is a common investigative tool.
