Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average cost of cleaning services in Austin?

The cost of cleaning services in Austin can vary significantly depending on the size of your home or office, the frequency of cleaning, and the scope of services required. Generally, you can expect to pay anywhere from $100 to $400+ per cleaning visit. Deep cleaning, move-in/move-out cleaning, and commercial spaces can be at the higher end of the scale. It's always a good idea to get a quote tailored to your specific needs.

How do I choose the best cleaning service for my needs?

Consider the following when selecting a cleaning service: read reviews, check for licensing and insurance, compare pricing, and find out what services are offered. Does the company offer the specialized cleaning that you need? Are green cleaning options available? Do they use safe and effective products? Prioritize companies that guarantee their work and have great customer service.

What types of cleaning services do Austin cleaning companies offer?

Most Austin cleaning companies offer standard residential cleaning, including dusting, vacuuming, mopping, and bathroom cleaning. Many offer deep cleaning services for areas like kitchens and bathrooms. Some also provide specialized services like move-in/move-out cleaning, commercial cleaning, and window cleaning. Some may offer eco-friendly or green cleaning options.

Are cleaning services in Austin insured and bonded?

Reputable cleaning services in Austin are typically insured and bonded, protecting both the customers and the cleaning company in the event of accidents or damages. Always verify that the company you choose is properly insured before booking their service.

How do I prepare my home for a cleaning service?

To ensure the most effective cleaning service, we recommend tidying up before the cleaners arrive. Pick up any clutter, clear surfaces, and secure any valuables. This allows the cleaning professionals to focus on the cleaning tasks at hand and ensures the best possible results. You should give your cleaners clear access.

Do I need to be home when the cleaning service arrives?

This depends on your preference and the cleaning company's policies. Many companies allow for key pick-up or entry through a lockbox, so you don’t need to be present. Discuss this with the cleaning service beforehand. If you have any specific instructions or preferences, it is ideal to be available for the initial cleaning.

What are the benefits of hiring a professional cleaning service?

Hiring a cleaning service saves you time and energy. It provides you with a consistently clean home or office as an expert takes care of all the chores. Professional cleaners use professional products and techniques, which can lead to a deeper clean. It also gives you more free time, allowing you to focus on work and leisure activities.

How far in advance should I book a cleaning service?

It's best to book your cleaning service as far in advance as possible, particularly if you have specific scheduling needs or want service during peak seasons. Popular cleaning companies often have busy schedules, so booking a few weeks or even months in advance is recommended to ensure availability. For urgent situations, some companies may offer same-day or next-day service.

What if I'm not satisfied with the cleaning service?

Most reputable cleaning services offer a satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy with the service, let the company know immediately. Many cleaning services will offer to rectify the issue by returning to re-clean the areas, providing a discount, or, in some cases, offering a partial or full refund.

What are some questions to ask when hiring a cleaning service?

Inquire about insurance and what their process is. Ask about their screening process, what cleaning products they use, and if they have any green cleaning options. Ensure that the company is licensed and ask for customer references. Understand their cancellation policy and if they have any guarantees in place.
