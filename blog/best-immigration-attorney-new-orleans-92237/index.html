What is the difference between an immigration lawyer and an immigration consultant?

An immigration lawyer is a licensed attorney who has passed the bar exam and is authorized to provide legal advice and representation in court. An immigration consultant, on the other hand, is typically not a lawyer and can only provide general information or assist with paperwork. They cannot provide legal advice. It is always recommended to work with a licensed immigration attorney for legal matters.
