Players bet on whether the "Player" or "Banker" hand will have a total closer to nine, or if it will be a tie. The game is largely based on chance, with minimal player decision-making involved after the initial bet.
  • Description: A popular card game where players bet on the outcome of two hands, the Player and the Banker, aiming for a total closest to nine.
