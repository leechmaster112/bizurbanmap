FAQ

Here are some frequently asked questions about permanent Christmas lights in Frisco, TX:

1. What are the benefits of permanent Christmas lights?

Permanent Christmas lights offer convenience, eliminating the need to put up and take down temporary lights annually. They provide a customizable and elegant look year-round and provide considerable cost savings over time.

2. How long do permanent Christmas lights last?

High-quality LED permanent Christmas lights can last for many years, often 10+ years, offering a long-term lighting solution.

3. Are permanent Christmas lights energy efficient?

Yes, most permanent Christmas lights utilize LED technology, which is highly energy-efficient, often using significantly less electricity than traditional incandescent lights.

4. How are permanent Christmas lights installed?

Installation varies, but typically involves professional installation by experienced technicians who mount the lights along the roofline, eaves, or other architectural features.

5. Can I control the lights with my phone?

Many modern permanent Christmas light systems, including those offered by Astoria Lighting Co - Frisco, provide user-friendly mobile app control for color changes, patterns, and scheduling.

Absolutely! The customizable nature of these lights means you can create custom displays for holidays, game days, and celebrations or simply use them for elegant ambient lighting.
