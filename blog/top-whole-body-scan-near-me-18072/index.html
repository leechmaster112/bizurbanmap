Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is a whole body scan? A whole body scan is an imaging test that uses advanced technology to create detailed pictures of the inside of your body.
  • What can a whole body scan detect? Whole body scans can help detect a wide range of potential health issues, including early-stage cancers, cardiovascular disease, and musculoskeletal problems.
  • Are whole body scans safe? The safety of whole body scans, which typically involve radiation exposure, depends on the type of scan, the frequency of scans, and your individual health profile, so discussing the risks and benefits with a healthcare professional is important.
  • How long does a whole body scan take? The duration of a whole body scan varies depending on the type of scan and the specific areas being scanned, but most scans take between 30 minutes to a couple of hours.
  • How do I prepare for a whole body scan? Preparation instructions will vary depending on the facility and the type of scan you're having. You may be asked to fast, avoid wearing metal, and inform the technician of any medical conditions or medications you're taking.
  • What happens after a whole body scan? After the scan, a radiologist will review the images and generate a report. You'll receive the results, usually within a few days, and your doctor will discuss any findings with you.
  • How much does a whole body scan cost? The cost of a whole body scan can vary widely depending on the provider, the type of scan, and your location. Prices can range from hundreds to thousands of dollars.
  • Is a whole body scan covered by insurance? Insurance coverage for whole body scans varies. Many insurance plans may not cover them if the scan is not deemed medically necessary, so it's a good idea to check with your insurance provider.
  • What types of technology are used in a whole body scan? Whole body scans use a variety of imaging technologies, including MRI (magnetic resonance imaging), CT (computed tomography) scans, and PET (positron emission tomography) scans.
  • Where can I find a "whole body scan near me?" To find a whole body scan near you, you can search online, ask your doctor for recommendations, or look for imaging centers or hospitals in your area.
  • Does Advanced Body Scan offer whole body scans? Yes, Advanced Body Scan offers comprehensive whole body scans with advanced imaging technology. Visit https://advancedbodyscan.com/ for more information.
  • When should I consider getting a whole body scan? You might want to consider a whole body scan if you have a family history of certain diseases, are experiencing symptoms, or are simply looking for a proactive approach to your health. Discuss your individual circumstances with your doctor.
