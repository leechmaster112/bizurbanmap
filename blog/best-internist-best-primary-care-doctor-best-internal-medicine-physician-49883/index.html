Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between an internist and a primary care doctor?
An internist is a physician specializing in internal medicine, which focuses on the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of diseases in adults. Many primary care doctors are internists, but primary care can also include family practitioners who treat all ages.
How do I choose the best internist for my needs?
Consider factors like physician qualifications, practice philosophy, patient reviews, location, and whether they specialize in areas relevant to your health. A good internist will prioritize open communication and personalized care.
What services does a primary care doctor typically provide?
Primary care doctors offer a wide range of services, including routine check-ups, health screenings, vaccinations, management of chronic conditions like diabetes and hypertension, and treatment for acute illnesses. They serve as your first point of contact for most health concerns.
How important is it to have a primary care physician?
It is highly important. A primary care physician (PCP) coordinates your overall healthcare, helps manage chronic conditions, performs preventive screenings to catch issues early, and guides you to specialists when needed. This continuity of care leads to better health outcomes.
When should I see an internal medicine physician versus a general practitioner?
An internal medicine physician is best for adults when you need in-depth diagnosis and management of complex medical conditions affecting internal organs. A general practitioner (GP) may offer broader care but might refer you to an internist for specialized adult internal medicine needs.
What makes a primary care physician "best"?
The "best" primary care doctor is subjective but generally includes excellent communication skills, a thorough and evidence-based approach to care, availability for appointments, compassion, and the ability to build a trusting long-term relationship with patients.
Can an internist manage chronic diseases effectively?
Yes, internists are extensively trained to manage a wide array of chronic diseases such as heart disease, diabetes, asthma, arthritis, and many others that affect adults. They focus on long-term health management and disease progression.
What are the benefits of finding a dedicated internal medicine physician?
A dedicated internal medicine physician offers specialized knowledge of adult diseases, preventive care strategies tailored for adults, and a deep understanding of how various conditions can interact. This specialization often leads to more accurate diagnoses and effective treatment plans.
How can I find a top-rated internist near me in 2026?
Start by looking at practices recommended by family and friends, checking online reviews and physician profiles, and exploring directories of board-certified internists. Consider visiting a practice like Internal Medicine Associates of Plano for a consultation.
Is it better to choose a large practice or a small practice for internal medicine?
Both have pros and cons. Large practices may offer more specialists and convenient hours, while smaller practices often provide a more personal touch and direct relationship with the physician. Internal Medicine Associates of Plano balances comprehensive care with personal attention.
