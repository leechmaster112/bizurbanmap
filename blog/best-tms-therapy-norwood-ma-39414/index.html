Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is TMS therapy, and how does it work?

Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) therapy is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic pulses to stimulate nerve cells in the brain, helping alleviate symptoms of depression and other mental health conditions. These magnetic pulses target specific areas of the brain involved in mood regulation, aiming to improve communication between brain cells and reduce depressive symptoms.

Is TMS therapy safe?

Yes, TMS therapy is generally considered safe. It is a non-invasive procedure with minimal side effects for most patients. The most common side effects are mild and temporary, such as headaches or scalp discomfort.

Who is a good candidate for TMS therapy?

TMS therapy is often recommended for individuals who have not found relief from depression with traditional treatments like medication or psychotherapy. It can also be effective for other conditions, such as anxiety disorders and OCD. A thorough evaluation by a mental health professional is necessary to determine if TMS is the right treatment option for you.

How long does a TMS therapy session last?

A typical TMS therapy session lasts between 20 to 60 minutes. The exact duration of a session can vary depending on the specific protocol being used and the individual needs of the patient.

How many TMS therapy sessions are needed?

The number of TMS sessions required typically ranges from 20 to 30 sessions, administered over several weeks. The treatment plan is often customized based on the patient's response to therapy and the severity of their condition. Some clinics may offer accelerated protocols.

What are the side effects of TMS therapy?

The most common side effects of TMS therapy are mild and temporary. These can include headaches, scalp discomfort, or tingling sensations at the site of stimulation. Serious side effects are rare, but it is important to discuss any concerns with your healthcare provider.

How much does TMS therapy cost?

The cost of TMS therapy can vary depending on the provider, the number of sessions, and the patient's insurance coverage. Many insurance plans cover TMS therapy, but it is essential to verify your coverage and any out-of-pocket expenses with your insurance provider. You can contact providers in the Norwood, MA area, such as Onward Psychiatry, for pricing information.

What should I expect during a TMS therapy session?

During a TMS therapy session, you will sit in a comfortable chair while a trained technician positions a magnetic coil near your head. The coil delivers short magnetic pulses to the targeted area of your brain. You will likely hear clicking sounds during the procedure, and you may feel a tapping sensation on your scalp. You will remain awake and alert throughout the session and will be able to communicate with the technician.

How successful is TMS therapy?

TMS therapy has demonstrated significant success in treating depression and other mental health conditions. Research studies have shown that a substantial number of patients experience a reduction in symptoms, and some achieve complete remission. The effectiveness of TMS can vary depending on individual factors and the condition being treated.
