Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How far in advance should I book a wedding florist in Pittsburgh?

A: It's best to book your florist as early as possible, ideally 9-12 months before your wedding date, especially if you are getting married during peak season (May-October) in 2025.

Q: What factors influence the cost of wedding flowers?

A: Several factors affect the cost, including flower types, seasonality, complexity of the designs, size of the arrangements, and labor costs.

Q: What questions should I ask potential wedding florists during a consultation?

A: Ask about their experience, style, flower sourcing, pricing structure, availability, and what services they provide beyond the floral arrangements.

Q: Can I provide inspiration photos to my wedding florist?

A: Absolutely! Inspiration photos are highly encouraged. They help the florist understand your vision and preferences.

Q: Do florists in Pittsburgh offer consultations?

A: Yes, most florists in Pittsburgh offer consultations. It's an essential step to discuss your wedding details and floral needs.

Q: How important is it to choose a florist who understands my wedding style?

A: Very important. The florist should be able to translate your vision into reality, whether it's classic, modern, rustic, or bohemian.

Q: How do I determine my wedding flower budget?

A: Consider your overall wedding budget and allocate a percentage (usually 8-10%) for flowers. Discuss your budget with potential florists to find options that fit.

Q: Can a florist help with other aspects of wedding décor?

A: Some florists, like Lilac & Fawn, offer event styling services to create a cohesive look, including table settings, linens, and other decorative elements.

Q: What are some popular flower choices for Pittsburgh weddings?

A: Popular choices include roses, peonies, hydrangeas, seasonal blooms, and locally sourced flowers. The best choices depend on the time of year and your personal style.

Q: What is the typical availability of a wedding florist during peak months in Pittsburgh?

A: Florists are often booked months or even a year in advance for peak wedding months (May-October). Early booking is crucial.

Q: Should I worry about the sustainability of my wedding flowers?

A: Many florists are now focusing on sustainability. Ask about flower sourcing and the option of using locally grown flowers. Lilac & Fawn is committed to this practice.
