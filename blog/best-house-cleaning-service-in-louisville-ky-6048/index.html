Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is the average cost of house cleaning services in Louisville, KY, in 2025?

A: The average cost can vary based on factors like the size of your home, the services included, and the frequency of cleaning. However, you can generally expect to pay somewhere between $100 and $300+ per cleaning session.

Q2: What services do house cleaning companies in Louisville typically offer?

A: Most cleaning services offer standard cleaning (dusting, vacuuming, mopping, bathroom cleaning, kitchen cleaning), deep cleaning (includes more detailed work), move-in/move-out cleaning, and sometimes specialized services like window washing or carpet cleaning.

Q3: How do I choose the best house cleaning service for my needs?

A: Consider your budget, the specific services you need, and the size of your home. Read reviews, compare prices, and check for insurance and bonding. Make sure the company offers a satisfaction guarantee.

Q4: Are house cleaning services in Louisville insured and bonded?

A: Reputable cleaning services in Louisville should be insured and bonded. This protects you in case of any damage or theft while the cleaners are in your home. Always ask about insurance before hiring a company.

Q5: How often should I schedule house cleaning services?

A: The frequency of cleaning depends on your lifestyle and needs. Many people opt for weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly cleaning. For busy households, weekly cleaning is often a good option, while less frequent cleaning might suffice for smaller homes or those with fewer residents.

Q6: What is included in a deep cleaning service?

A: Deep cleaning typically includes everything in a standard cleaning plus more in-depth tasks, such as cleaning inside appliances (oven, refrigerator), cleaning baseboards, window sills, and more. It targets areas that are often missed in routine cleanings.

Q7: Do house cleaning services in Louisville use green or eco-friendly cleaning products?

A: Many house cleaning services in Louisville offer eco-friendly cleaning options. Be sure to ask about the products they use and if they are certified as sustainable or safe.

Q8: How far in advance should I book my house cleaning service?

A: It's best to book your cleaning service as early as possible, particularly for recurring services or during peak seasons. Booking at least a week or two in advance is recommended. For specific services like move-in/move-out cleans, booking several weeks out is advised to get your desired date.

Q9: Can I customize my house cleaning service to fit my specific needs?

A: Many house cleaning services in Louisville offer customizable plans. Consider what cleaning tasks are most important to you and ask companies if they can tailor their services to accommodate them.

Q10: Are there any services for move-in/move-out cleaning in Louisville?

A: Yes, many cleaning services, like Scrub Lou, offer move-in/move-out cleaning services, which are perfect for preparing a new home or cleaning a previous residence.

Q11: How do I prepare my home for a cleaning service?

A: Decluttering the space is very important. Putting away items where possible, such as clothes, toys and other objects, greatly helps the cleaning crew. Removing personal items and valuables can also help the process.

Q12: Is it necessary to tip my house cleaner in Louisville, KY?

A: Tipping is customary in the house cleaning industry. It's up to you, but showing your appreciation with 15-20% is recommended. It can be more, depending on the quality of service.
