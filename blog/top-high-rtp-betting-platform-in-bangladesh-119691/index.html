RTP stands for Return to Player. It's a theoretical percentage indicating how much of the total money wagered on a particular game (like a slot machine) is paid back to players over a long period. A higher RTP means a lower house edge, generally offering better value to the bettor.

For slot games, an RTP of 96% or higher is generally considered good. For other casino games, a lower house edge (which corresponds to a higher RTP) is always preferable. Platforms focusing on player value will aim to offer games with RTPs in this favorable range.

How does RTP differ from volatility in slot games?

RTP (Return to Player) is the long-term theoretical payout percentage, while volatility (or variance) refers to the risk associated with a slot game. High volatility games pay out less frequently but can offer larger wins, while low volatility games pay out more often but with smaller wins. Both are important factors to consider alongside RTP.
