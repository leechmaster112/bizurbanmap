Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some frequently asked questions about dental implants in Langley:

  1. What are dental implants? Dental implants are artificial tooth roots that are surgically placed into the jawbone to support replacement teeth, such as crowns, bridges, or dentures.
  2. How long do dental implants last? With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime.
  3. Am I a candidate for dental implants in Langley? You are likely a good candidate if you have missing teeth, healthy gums, and sufficient bone density in your jaw. A consultation with a dentist will determine your suitability.
  4. What is the dental implant procedure like? The procedure typically involves the placement of the implant, a healing period for osseointegration (fusion with the bone), and the placement of the abutment and crown.
  5. How much do dental implants cost in Langley? The cost of dental implants varies depending on several factors, including the number of implants, the material used, and the complexity of the case. Costs can vary between different dental facilities.
  6. Are dental implants painful? The implant procedure is usually performed under local anesthesia, so you should not feel any pain. Some patients may experience some discomfort after the procedure.
  7. What are the benefits of dental implants? Dental implants offer numerous benefits, including improved chewing ability, enhanced appearance, and the prevention of bone loss. They also provide a durable and long-lasting solution for missing teeth.
  8. What is the best type of dental implant? The "best" type of dental implant depends on your individual needs and circumstances. Your dentist will recommend the most suitable option for your situation.
  9. How should I go about getting dental implants? The first step is to schedule a consultation with a qualified dentist in Langley who specializes in implant dentistry.
  10. How do I find a good dental implant specialist in Langley? Look for a dentist with extensive experience, positive reviews, and advanced training in implantology. Ask about the technology used in the practice, and their approach to patient care.
  11. What if I have bone loss and want dental implants? Bone grafting procedures can often be performed to build up the bone and make you a suitable candidate for implants. Your dentist will evaluate your situation and discuss your options.
