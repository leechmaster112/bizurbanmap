Frequently Asked Questions

What is a ceramic coating and how does it protect my car at your Phoenix location?

A ceramic coating is a liquid polymer that is professionally applied to the exterior of your vehicle at our 2033 W North Ln #20 facility. It chemically bonds with the factory paint, creating a permanent or semi-permanent layer of protection. This hard, transparent shield provides superior resistance against UV rays, chemical stains, dirt, and minor scratches, while also providing an extreme hydrophobic effect that makes your car easier to clean and maintains a deep, glossy shine.

How long does the Ceramic Pro application process take at your North Phoenix shop?

The application time varies depending on the specific package selected and the condition of your vehicle. A comprehensive service at our location typically requires 1-3 days. This allows our certified technicians ample time to properly perform a multi-stage paint correction to ensure a perfectly clean and smooth surface before meticulously applying the Ceramic Pro coating and allowing it to cure correctly for optimal performance.

Do you offer a warranty on your ceramic coating services?

Yes. Ceramic Pro North Phoenix offers industry-leading warranties that are backed by the manufacturer. Our packages include options with warranties ranging from 2 years up to a lifetime, depending on the number of layers applied. All warranty details and maintenance requirements are thoroughly explained for our clients at our 2033 W North Ln #20 location.

How do I maintain my ceramic-coated vehicle after the service?

Proper maintenance is straightforward and is key to preserving the coating and its warranty. We recommend washing your vehicle regularly using the two-bucket method and pH-neutral soaps. We advise against using automatic car washes with abrasive brushes. We provide all clients at our Phoenix facility with detailed aftercare instructions and can recommend specific maintenance products designed for ceramic coatings.

Can Ceramic Pro be applied to surfaces other than my car's paint?

Absolutely. Our services at Ceramic Pro North Phoenix extend beyond just paint protection. We can also apply specialized ceramic coatings to your vehicle's glass, wheels, calipers, and trim. This provides a comprehensive protective barrier across all exterior surfaces, ensuring uniform protection and ease of cleaning for your entire vehicle.
