Our mission is simple: to empower children to communicate effectively, express themselves clearly, and build the essential social and learning skills they need to thrive. We achieve this through a comprehensive range of evidence-based assessment and therapy services delivered with warmth, expertise, and a deep understanding of child development.

At Articulate Kids, we specialize in addressing a wide variety of speech and language challenges. Our services are designed to help children overcome difficulties with:

Articulation and Phonology: Clearing up unclear speech sounds. Language Development: Building vocabulary, grammar, comprehension, and expressive language skills. Receptive and Expressive Language: Understanding spoken and written language and effectively using language to communicate. Fluency: Assisting children with stuttering or disfluency. Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC): Supporting children who need alternative ways to communicate, including the use of speech-generating devices. Stuttering: Providing strategies and therapy to manage and reduce stuttering. Pragmatic Language: Developing social communication skills, understanding non-literal language, and navigating social interactions.
