Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of vein treatments do you offer at your Brick location?

We provide a comprehensive range of treatments including sclerotherapy, endovenous laser treatment (EVLT), radiofrequency ablation (RFA), and surface laser treatments for spider veins and rosacea. Our treatments are tailored to address both symptomatic varicose veins and cosmetic concerns.

Q2: Is the treatment process painful?

Most of our procedures are performed with local anesthesia or numbing creams to ensure your comfort. While some mild tenderness or bruising may occur after treatment, significant pain is uncommon.

Q3: How long does a typical vein treatment session take?

The duration varies depending on the complexity of the case, but most treatment sessions at our Brick clinic range from 30 minutes to a few hours. Your physician will provide a more specific estimate during your consultation.

Q4: Are the results of vein treatment permanent?

While results are typically excellent and long-lasting, it's possible for new veins to develop over time due to genetics or lifestyle factors. Regular follow-up care and maintaining a healthy weight can help preserve results.

Q5: What should I expect during my initial consultation at Metro Vein Centers Brick?

Your initial visit will include a thorough medical history review, a physical examination of your legs, and possibly an ultrasound to map your veins. Our physician will then discuss your options, answer your questions, and develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to your needs.
