Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) therapy is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic pulses to stimulate nerve cells in the brain. Specifically, it targets areas of the brain that are underactive in individuals with conditions such as major depressive disorder. This targeted stimulation can help to rebalance brain activity, leading to significant improvements in mood, energy levels, and overall quality of life. Unlike some other treatment options, TMS therapy is generally well-tolerated and does not involve systemic side effects often associated with medications.
