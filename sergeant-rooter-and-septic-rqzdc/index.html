Frequently Asked Questions

1. How often should I get my septic tank pumped?

Generally, most septic tanks require pumping every 3 to 5 years, depending on tank size, number of occupants, water usage, and the amount of solids entering the system. Regular pumping prevents buildup that can clog the drain field and cause system failure.

2. What should I do if I have a major drain clog?

If you experience a severe drain clog, especially one that fills the pipe or affects multiple drains, please call us immediately at (478) 324-9799. We offer emergency drain services for Perry residents and can often arrive quickly to resolve the issue.

3. Do you offer emergency services outside of regular business hours?

Absolutely. Being "always on duty," we understand plumbing emergencies can happen anytime. Our team is prepared to respond to your call for septic pumping or drain cleaning services in Perry, GA, 24/7, 365 days a year.

4. How long does a typical septic tank pumping take?

The duration of a septic tank pumping service can vary based on the tank size and the amount of sludge present. Typically, a standard residential pumping job takes between 30 minutes to an hour. Our team will confirm the expected time upon arrival.

5. Are your drain cleaning services effective for all types of clogs?

Yes. We utilize a range of professional drain cleaning techniques and specialized equipment designed to handle various types of blockages, from minor hair clogs to grease buildup and more stubborn mineral deposits common in Perry's plumbing systems.
