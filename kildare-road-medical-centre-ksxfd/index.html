Location

Kildare Road Medical Centre, located at 36 Kildare Road, Blacktown, NSW, 2148, has been a cornerstone of healthcare in the Blacktown community since its doors opened in April 2010. Following a comprehensive refurbishment, the centre was established to provide high-quality, patient-focused medical services within a modern and comfortable environment. Situated conveniently on Kildare Road, the Medical Centre is dedicated to serving the residents of Blacktown and surrounding immediate areas, offering a wide range of general medical care and specialised health services right here in our local community.

At Kildare Road Medical Centre, we pride ourselves on delivering comprehensive primary healthcare tailored to the needs of our patients. Our team of experienced and compassionate doctors is committed to providing thorough and individualised medical attention. We understand that visiting a doctor can sometimes be stressful, and we strive to make each patient visit as comfortable and efficient as possible.

Our Services

Kildare Road Medical Centre offers a broad spectrum of general medical services designed to meet the health needs of our diverse patient base. Our general practitioners (GPs) handle a wide array of health concerns, from routine check-ups and minor ailments to the management of chronic conditions. We provide comprehensive health assessments, including health screenings and lifestyle counselling, to help our patients maintain optimal health and prevent future illnesses.

We are equipped to manage common illnesses such as colds, flu, respiratory infections, and skin conditions. Our facility includes a well-equipped examination room and a laboratory for basic pathology testing, allowing us to provide many diagnostic services on-site. We also offer minor procedures, such as wound care and suturing, to address minor injuries promptly.

Specialised Care

In addition to general medical practice, Kildare Road Medical Centre collaborates with various healthcare professionals to provide access to specialised care when needed. We have strong referral pathways to specialists in fields such as cardiology, dermatology, neurology, and orthopaedics, ensuring our patients receive the appropriate level of care efficiently.

We also focus on preventive healthcare and chronic disease management. Our GPs work closely with patients diagnosed with long-term conditions such as diabetes, hypertension, and asthma to create personalised management plans. We provide ongoing monitoring, education, and support to help patients effectively manage their conditions and improve their quality of life.

Health Information and Resources

Recognising the importance of patient education, Kildare Road Medical Centre offers a range of health information resources. These materials cover common health topics, preventive measures, and information on managing specific conditions. Our aim is to empower patients with knowledge to make informed decisions about their health.

We also provide immunisation services, including influenza and COVID-19 vaccinations, following government guidelines and recommendations. Our team administers these vaccines safely and efficiently, contributing to the community's efforts in disease prevention.

Accessibility and Convenience

Conveniently located at 36 Kildare Road, Blacktown, our Medical Centre is easily accessible by public transport and private vehicles. We are committed to making healthcare accessible and convenient for our patients. Frequently Asked Questions

1. What services does Kildare Road Medical Centre offer?

Kildare Road Medical Centre provides a comprehensive range of general medical services, including routine check-ups, management of acute and chronic illnesses, minor procedures, immunisations, and health screenings. We also offer chronic disease management plans and referrals to specialists when necessary.

2. Do you accept bulk-billed patients?

Yes, Kildare Road Medical Centre accepts bulk-billed patients for eligible services under Medicare. Please discuss your entitlement with our reception staff when booking your appointment.

3. What are your operating hours?

Our centre operates from Monday to Friday, typically opening at 8:30 AM and closing at 5:00 PM. We strive to accommodate patients' schedules, but it's always best to confirm appointment times with our friendly team.

4. Can I request an appointment outside of regular business hours?

While we primarily operate during standard business hours, we may be able to accommodate after-hours requests for urgent medical concerns. Please contact our centre directly to inquire about emergency appointments.

5. How do I book an appointment?

Appointments can be booked by calling our office at (02) 8822 3000. You can also find contact information and basic details on our website at https://www.kildaremedical.com.au/. Walk-ins are sometimes accommodated, but scheduling an appointment ensures you receive timely care.
