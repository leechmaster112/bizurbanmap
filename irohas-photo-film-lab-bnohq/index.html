Services Offered at 398-402 Sussex St

At our Haymarket location, we specialise in a comprehensive range of film processing services tailored to meet the needs of our valued clients:

• Precision Film Developing: We offer a wide array of film types, from classic black and white films like Ilford FP4+ and Kodak Tri-X, to vibrant colour negatives such as Fujifilm Neopan and Kodak Portra. Our state-of-the-art processors ensure consistent, high-quality results, accurately capturing the tones, contrasts, and colours unique to each film's potential.

• Expert Film Scanning: Once your film is safely and expertly processed, we provide professional film scanning services. Our calibrated drum scanners and advanced software allow us to digitise your images with exceptional clarity, preserving the fine details and dynamic range achieved in the darkroom. We can match scans to your specific film type and desired output (screen viewing, printing, or archival).

• C-41 Colour Film Processing: For those who favour the timeless look of C-41 colour slide film (like Kodachrome and E6 films), we offer dedicated processing facilities. We handle the complex chemical baths required to bring out the rich colours and characteristic look of these films.

• Black & White Print Finishing: While our core strength lies in film processing, we also offer high-quality black and white print finishing for 35mm and 120/220 roll film. We utilise professional enlargers and premium papers to produce archival-grade prints.

• Film Developing Consultation: Unsure about your film type or desired development settings? Our knowledgeable team is here to assist. We can provide expert advice on film handling, optimal development times, and techniques to help you achieve the best possible results from your rolls.
