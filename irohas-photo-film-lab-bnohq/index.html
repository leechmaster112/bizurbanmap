Location

Irohas Photo Film Lab, located at 398-402 Sussex St, Haymarket, NSW, 2000, stands as a premier destination for discerning clients seeking top-tier film photography services right in the heart of Sydney's vibrant CBD. We pride ourselves on offering a comprehensive suite of professional film processing and related services, catering specifically to the needs of our valued Haymarket neighbours and the wider Sydney community who choose to bring their film to our dedicated lab.

Nestled in the bustling atmosphere of Haymarket, our facility provides a unique blend of traditional darkroom expertise and modern processing technology. We understand that film photography is more than just a medium; it's an art form, a craft, and for many, a deeply personal connection to the photographic process. Our commitment is to uphold the integrity of your film, ensuring that every roll is handled with the utmost care, precision, and respect from start to finish.

At Irohas Photo Film Lab, we specialise in a full range of film development services. Whether you favour the timeless grain of black and white film, the vibrant colours of slide film, or the nuanced tones of colour negative film, our experienced technicians are adept at processing a wide variety of formats. We handle standard 35mm film with ease, but our expertise extends to medium format films such as 120 and 220 roll film, known for their larger negatives and potential for stunning detail. We also cater to the specific requirements of infrared film, ensuring the unique tonal shifts and atmospheric effects characteristic of this medium are preserved accurately.

Our processing capabilities are complemented by a range of finishing options designed to enhance the final look of your prints. We offer standard C-41 colour processing for compatibility with virtually all colour negative films, followed by optional steps like hand washing for increased archival stability or the application of professional-grade fixatives. For black and white film, our lab is equipped for the traditional and reliable D-76 or Kodak's HC-110 processes, yielding rich contrasts and deep blacks. We also provide film scanning services, transforming your physical negatives and slides into high-resolution digital files, perfect for sharing online, printing with modern techniques, or archiving for the future. This service allows you to bypass traditional printing methods entirely if preferred.

While our core business revolves around film processing, we also offer essential ancillary services to support your entire photographic journey. We can supply a wide selection of photographic films, emulsions, and papers, ensuring you have access to quality materials right here in Haymarket. Our knowledgeable team can provide expert advice on film choice, developing techniques, and troubleshooting common issues, helping you make the most of your photographic endeavours. We are here not just to process your film, but to be a resource for film photography enthusiasts operating from or visiting the Sussex St area.

Conveniently situated at 398-402 Sussex St, our location places us within easy reach of the central business district, making us an accessible hub for film processing.
