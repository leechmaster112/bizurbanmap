Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What brake services do you offer at your Raleigh location?

A1: At our Raleigh shop, we offer a comprehensive range of brake services, including inspections, pad and shoe replacement, rotor and drum resurfacing, caliper replacement, and master cylinder service. We diagnose and repair any issues related to your vehicle's braking system.

Q2: How often should I get a wheel alignment?

A2: It's generally recommended to get a wheel alignment every 6,000 to 10,000 miles or as part of your regular oil change service. However, signs like uneven tire wear, a pulling sensation while driving, or the check engine light illuminating may indicate a need for an alignment sooner.

Q3: What is the difference between a wheel alignment and a tire alignment?

A3: "Wheel alignment" is the standard term used by professionals. It refers to adjusting the angles of your wheels to the manufacturer's specifications. "Tire alignment" is sometimes used interchangeably, but it's the same process focused on ensuring your tires wear evenly and your vehicle handles correctly.

Q4: Can you do brake services on foreign cars or luxury vehicles?

A4: Yes, our technicians are experienced with a wide variety of vehicles, including domestic, import, and luxury brands. We use diagnostic tools and parts compatible with your specific make and model to ensure quality service.

Q5: How long does a typical brake repair or alignment service take?

A5: The time required depends on the specific service needed. Simple adjustments or minor part replacements might take 30-60 minutes, while more complex brake repairs could take longer. Our team will provide you with an estimate based on your vehicle's needs.
